# Contributing to the Titanfall 2 Handbook

Thanks for helping improve the Titanfall 2 Handbook!

The Handbook is a community-maintained project, and contributions are welcome.

## What you can contribute

You can help by:

- Correcting inaccurate information.
- Adding missing mechanics, techniques, or map information.
- Improving explanations and clarity.
- Fixing spelling, grammar, or formatting.
- Adding useful references and sources.
- Suggesting improvements to the structure of the Handbook.
- Helping translate the Handbook into other languages.

## How to submit a contribution

All contributions are reviewed by the project maintainer before they become part of the official Handbook.

The general process is:

1. Find the page or content you would like to improve.
2. Create a new branch in the GitHub repository for your changes.
3. Make your changes in that branch.
4. Open a Pull Request against the `main` branch.
5. Explain what you changed and why.
6. The project maintainer will review your contribution.
7. The maintainer may request changes or make edits to the contribution.
8. Once approved, the Pull Request may be merged into `main`.

You do not need permission before submitting a contribution.

Submitting a Pull Request does not guarantee that the contribution will be accepted. Contributions are reviewed to make sure they are accurate, useful, and appropriate for the Handbook.

## Before contributing

Please make sure that:

1. Your contribution is relevant to Titanfall 2.
2. Information is accurate and, where appropriate, supported by reliable sources or testing.
3. You respect the copyrights and trademarks of Electronic Arts, Respawn Entertainment, and other rights holders.
4. You do not submit material that you do not have permission to redistribute.

## Pull requests

For substantial changes, please open a pull request describing:

- What you changed.
- Why you changed it.
- Any sources or testing supporting the change.

Small corrections and improvements are welcome.

## Content and conduct

Please be respectful and constructive toward other contributors and users.

See `CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md` for the project's community standards.

## Questions and suggestions

If you're unsure whether something belongs in the Handbook, feel free to open an issue and discuss it before making a larger contribution.
