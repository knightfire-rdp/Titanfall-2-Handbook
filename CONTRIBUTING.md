# Contributing to the Titanfall 2 Handbook

Thanks to you a lot for helping improve the Titanfall 2 Handbook!

The Handbook is a community-maintained project, and contributions are welcome. They will thoroughly inspected and edited if needed.

## What you can contribute

You can help by:

- Correcting inaccurate information.
- Adding missing mechanics, techniques, or map information.
- Improving explanations and clarity.
- Fixing spelling, grammar, or wording (English is my 2nd language :C).
- Adding useful references and sources.
- Suggesting improvements to the structure of the Handbook.
- Adding short videos made strictly for the explanation/mechanic (please, I find it the most tedious to record and edit videos).

## Before contributing

Please make sure that:

1. Your contribution is relevant to Titanfall 2.
2. Information is accurate and, where appropriate, supported by reliable sources or testing.
3. You try to follow the preexisting Handbook style and formating (I can help change it if you're unsure).
4. You respect the copyrights and trademarks of Electronic Arts, Respawn Entertainment, and other rights holders.
5. You do not submit material that you do not have permission to redistribute.

## Pull requests

For substantial changes, please open a pull request describing:

- What you changed
- Why you changed it
- Any sources or testing supporting the change

Small corrections and improvements are welcome.

## Content and conduct

Please be respectful toward other contributors and users. Contributions should focus on improving the Handbook as a useful resource for the Titanfall 2 community.

See the project's `CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md` for the community standards.

## Questions and suggestions

If you're unsure whether something belongs in the Handbook, feel free to open an issue and discuss it before making a larger contribution.
