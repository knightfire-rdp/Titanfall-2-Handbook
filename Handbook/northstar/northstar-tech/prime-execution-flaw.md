# 🔻 Prime Execution Flaw

Weird bug where Ion can catch Northstar's Flight Core rockets that she launches at an enemy Titan during her Prime execution. The execution Flight Core rockets don't deal damage and have no energy drain effect, but for some reason Vortex Shield can reflect said rockets, making them deal the regular Flight Core damage. So you can do funny stuff like this here.
