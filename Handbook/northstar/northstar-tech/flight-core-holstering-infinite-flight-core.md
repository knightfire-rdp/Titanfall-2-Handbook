# ☣️ Flight Core Holstering (Infinite Flight Core)

Documenting this because Linuslover begged me to.

Bizarre bug that lets you equip Flight Core as an additional weapon and swap it out for a Railgun at will, so essentially an infinite Flight Core. Now that I think about it, “Infinite Flight Core” is quite a misleading name; a better name would be “Flight Core Holstering.” Yea, “Flight Core Holstering,” that's what I'll call it.&#x20;

&#x20;So basically, you can holster your Flight Core as a secondary weapon, but it requires you to use a macro. Yea, which is why I put a :biohazard: symbol on this page since I wouldn't consider glitches that require a macro as tech. The said macro is played via an autoexec file, and it essentially binds the “1” key to crouch, the “2” key to uncrouch, and the “3” key to crouch. You start Flight Core and spam the “1” and “2” keys, even after the core ends. Keep spamming said keys until a Pilot gives you enough batteries for the core, which is why this bug is documented because you need to get a 2nd core without being able to fire your gun. You'll know you're doing it properly because your Railgun and Flight Core crosshairs will constantly glitch out. Once your Pilot feeds you the questionable amount of batteries, start your 2nd Flight Core and immediately switch to spamming the “2” and “3” keys until your core ends. Once it ends, your Flight Core will be holstered, and you no longer need to spam the keys and can remove the macro. Press “1” to equip your Railgun, press “2” to equip your Flight Core, and that's it. The Flight Core is literally just the Flight Core weapon, so you can still dash and use abilities as normal.

The bug doesn't disappear after you disembark; it disappears when you eject or your Titan explodes.

### &#x20; <mark style="color:yellow;">Step-by-Step Guide</mark>

1. <mark style="color:yellow;">Get</mark> [Northstar Client.](https://northstar.tf/)
2. <mark style="color:yellow;">Then get</mark> [Vanilla Plus](https://thunderstore.io/c/northstar/p/NachosChipeados/VanillaPlus/) <mark style="color:yellow;">or</mark> [Ion Client](https://github.com/sonny-tel/Ion) <mark style="color:yellow;">to have access to the console.</mark> (Ion client is cooler; it lets you join people like via Origin.)
3. <mark style="color:yellow;">Get the 2 cfg files and put them in your Titanfall 2 directory folder, in the “Titanfall2 -> r2 -> cfg” folder.</mark> (Use Steam properties to find it.)
4. <mark style="color:yellow;">Start the game, get Flight Core, open the console via the “F2” key, and type “exec setupon” to activate the config.</mark>
5. <mark style="color:yellow;">Use Flight Core while spamming the “1” and “2” keys nonstop, even after the core ends.</mark> (You'll know whether you got it right if you see your Plasma Railgun crosshair constantly switch with the Flight Core crosshair.)
6. <mark style="color:yellow;">Have a teammate feed you batteries until you get 2nd core.</mark>
7. <mark style="color:yellow;">The moment you activate your 2nd core, you immediately switch to spamming the “2” and “3” keys.</mark>
8. <mark style="color:yellow;">Once your 2nd Flight Core ends, your Flight Core should be holstered. Make sure you press “2” to uncrouch and then you can now turn off the cfg by typing “exec setupoff” in the console.</mark>&#x20;
9. <mark style="color:yellow;">Now you have a holstered Flight Core; simply press “1” to switch to Railgun and press “2” to switch to Flight Core, and viola!</mark>
