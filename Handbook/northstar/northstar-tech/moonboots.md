# 🟢 Moonboots

## <mark style="color:yellow;">Moonboots</mark>

Moonboots is a tech that gives your Pilot 16% reduced gravity.&#x20;

To get this glitch, you must disembark during Hover or Flight Core, which normally isn't possible unless you're right above the ground. So hover below an object that prevents you from rising off the ground too much to be able to disembark.&#x20;

The glitch will end the moment you Hover or Flight Core again, but otherwise will persist even if you die or the round ends, yea, even if you switch your Titan. This glitch also affects you in your Titan, yes, [phase-jumping](../../ronin/ronin-tech/phase-movement-tech.md#phase-jump-zery-jump) too.

### <mark style="color:yellow;">Key Features</mark> <a href="#key-features" id="key-features"></a>

* <mark style="color:yellow;">Disembarking during Hover or Flight Core applies a 16% gravity reduction to your Pilot and Titan.</mark>
  * <mark style="color:yellow;">You can also disembark and immediately reembark.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Using Hover or Flight Core again removes moonboots.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Moonboots persists until the end of the match, even through death, LTS rounds, and Titan change.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">During the disembark you also briefly experience a hovering Pilot glitch too.</mark>

## <mark style="color:yellow;">Hovering Pilot Glitch</mark>

A funny byproduct of the Moonboots glitch. When you disembark during a Hover or Flight Core, your Pilot still continues the Northstar's flight animation until it ends. So yeah… you can fly as a Pilot, which is hilarious. For a longer effect, use Flight Core and disembark early.

<mark style="color:yellow;">Viper Thrusters do NOT make you hover farther or faster.</mark>
