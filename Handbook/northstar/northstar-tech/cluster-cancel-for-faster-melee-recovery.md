# 🟢 “Cluster Cancel” for Faster Melee Recovery

You can melee faster by canceling the Cluster Missile deployment with a melee. After firing a Railgun you normally have a delay before you can melee. But trying to deploy a Cluster Missile removes that delay and with that in mind you can melee before the Cluster Missile fires to cancel its deployment and save your Cluster Missile for an early melee.

### <mark style="color:yellow;">Step-by-Step Sequence</mark> <a href="#step-by-step-sequence" id="step-by-step-sequence"></a>

1. <mark style="color:yellow;">Fire your Railgun.</mark>
2. <mark style="color:yellow;">Activate Cluster Missile.</mark>
3. <mark style="color:yellow;">Melee right after you press the Cluster Missile key.</mark>
   1. (Your Cluster Missile gets canceled, but your melee still goes through.)
