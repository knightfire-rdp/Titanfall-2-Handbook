# ☣️ Spammable Firewall Sound

You can cancel the firewall with the weapon switch 1 key quickly and repeatedly, literally able to spam the Firewall deploy sound. You and everyone nearby can hear the sound effect.

What purpose does it have? No idea, but it might be used for something some day. You might as well use it behind walls near enemy Legions to have them do a 100-yard stare in real life upon hearing multiple Firewalls being deployed right beside them, thinking there are many Scorches right near them.

### <mark style="color:yellow;">**Step-by-Step Sequence**</mark>

1. <mark style="color:yellow;">Press the Firewall key</mark>
2. <mark style="color:yellow;">Press the primary weapon switch key immediately after</mark> (by default “1”)<mark style="color:yellow;">.</mark>
3. <mark style="color:yellow;">Your Firewall will be canceled and you'll play the Firewall sound effect.</mark>
