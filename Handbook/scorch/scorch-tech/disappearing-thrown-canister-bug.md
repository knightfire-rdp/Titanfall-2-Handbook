# 🔻 Disappearing Thrown Canister Bug

Very weird bug involving a canister disappearing inside of a Titan or bouncing off and disappearing into the floor. Similar to how a canister will disappear when it tries to land on top of another canister.&#x20;

From testing, it seems to only work against Legion and Scorch. I assume it's due to their vents having an odd hitbox that causes the canisters to clip in. But that doesn't explain why a canister can sometimes just bounce off a head and disappear despite that not working with other Titans. Doesn't seem to work from behind.

### <mark style="color:yellow;">Known Methods to Cause This Bug</mark>

* <mark style="color:yellow;">Aiming at the vent itself.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Aiming above the vent.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Aiming at the forehead.</mark>
