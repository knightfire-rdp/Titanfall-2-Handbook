# 🟢 Glow Gun Glitch

This is a niche, cool little trick that lets you glitch out your Predator Cannon to have a permanent Power Shot glow effect until you power-shot yourself. Surprisingly, the effect persists even when you fire, use Gun Shield, and change gun mode.&#x20;

This glitch has the use case of tripping up your opponents by never showing your real Power Shot glow animation. The only way for the enemy to react is via the audio cue of your Power Shot charging up. For them, it'll look like your Predator Cannon is always glowing, but for you, it won't glow at all.  So combine it with e-smoke or another form of loud noise to catch the enemy off guard.

You can combine it with the [Hold Power Shot glitch](auto-titan-hold-power-shot.md) too, but you need at least 2 Power Shots via. Hidden Compartment kit.

<mark style="color:yellow;">**To activate this glitch, you need to power-shot and immediately disembark and reembark.**</mark>

#### <mark style="color:yellow;">**Key Features**</mark> <a href="#step-by-step-sequence" id="step-by-step-sequence"></a>

* <mark style="color:yellow;">Power Shot glow effect on the gun until you either power-shot, eject, or even your Titan explodes.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Persists when firing normally, using Gun Shield, changing gun modes, and even disembarking.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">The Auto-Titan can have it too if you disembark, and it'll disappear if they power-shot.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">The enemy can only react via sound to a Power Shot.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">You can't see the glow yourself, only others can.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">It can be combined with the</mark> [Hold Power Shot glitch.](auto-titan-hold-power-shot.md)
