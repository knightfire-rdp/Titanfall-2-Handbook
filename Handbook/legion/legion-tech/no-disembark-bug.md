# 🔻 No Disembark Bug

Dying while being in the middle of firing a Power Shot or while having the [Hold Power Shot glitch](auto-titan-hold-power-shot.md) active will prevent you from disembarking in the next round of LTS, and when you get a new Titan.

Ejecting mid-Power Shot animation will not cause this bug.

### <mark style="color:yellow;">Causes for this Bug</mark>

* <mark style="color:yellow;">Dying during the Power Shot fire animation.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Having a round end during the Power Shot fire animation.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Dying or having a round end while</mark> [holding a Power Shot.](auto-titan-hold-power-shot.md)

### <mark style="color:yellow;">Fixes</mark>

* <mark style="color:yellow;">Power-shotting again will remove the bug.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Ejecting will remove the bug.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">The bug will disappear on its own after the match ends.</mark>

{% hint style="warning" %}
<mark style="color:red;">BE AWARE</mark> this bug does not go away when you switch Titans. So if you switch to a different Titan with this bug on you, you'll be unable to disembark untill you do one of those fixes.
{% endhint %}
