# 🔰 Hold Power Shot in Titan

:beginner:"Ability to store an extra Power Shot at the inability to shoot until you use said Power Shot."

## <mark style="color:yellow;">Hold Power Shot in Titan</mark>

Very similar to [auto-titan-hold-power-shot.md](auto-titan-hold-power-shot.md "mention"), but without disembark and with some RNG[^1] element to it. You have roughly a 40% chance of doing it successfully; it also requires very precise timing and is easier with higher ping. You basically let go of the ads key during the Power Shot animation and then press the Power Shot key just as the Power Shot is about to fire.

I spent nearly 2 hours shooting a wall in a private match with the only hint being “press right click during power shot animation” from Starspace.&#x20;

### <mark style="color:yellow;">Step-by-Step Sequence</mark> <a href="#step-by-step-sequence" id="step-by-step-sequence"></a>

1. <mark style="color:yellow;">Make sure you're running Hidden Compartment and have 2 Power Shots ready.</mark>
2. <mark style="color:yellow;">Have the gun fully spooled in and ready to shoot.</mark>
3. <mark style="color:yellow;">Press the Power Shot key.</mark>
4. <mark style="color:yellow;">Let go of the</mark> [<mark style="color:yellow;">ADS</mark>](#user-content-fn-2)[^2] <mark style="color:yellow;">key.</mark>
5. &#x20;<mark style="color:yellow;">Press the Power Shot key the moment you're about to fire the Power Shot.</mark>
   1. (Due to randomness, you might fire either 1 or 2 Power Shots instead of triggering the glitch.)
6. <mark style="color:yellow;">If you succeed, you'll fire the first Power Shot normally and store the 2nd one.</mark>

## <mark style="color:yellow;">Storing the First Power Shot Only</mark>

Very rarely, like a 3% chance, your Titan can fire the first Power Shot instead of firing it and storing the 2nd one. No actual idea how you can cause this to happen more often, but it can happen. Like above, I spent another hour trying to get this to work more consistently.
