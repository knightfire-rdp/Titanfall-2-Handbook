# 🟢 Invisible Reload Animation

If there is a time when Legion is most vulnerable, it's during his reload. Reload is by far the biggest time window for an enemy to punish him; a 4-second reload is quite punishing, especially since all of Legion's abilities are tied around his gun. He has no means of dealing damage outside of his gun filled with ammo, and sure, melee too, but melee is nothing compared to other Titans like Laser Shot or Thermal Shield, etc.

Sure, you can use Gun Shield to help block most of the damage, but good players will just dash into you and shoot through it. Sure, you can angle your Gun Shield towards the enemy to further mitigate said “shoot through it” method, but it's still a big liability to let the enemy know that your gun is empty. Thankfully there is a solution to this.

The solution is an invisible reload animation. The best way to avoid letting the enemy take advantage of your weakness is to prevent showing it in the first place. So the method is simple: **activate reload and then instantly activate Gun Shield.** The Gun Shield, for some reason, when activated during your reload animation, glitches it out and doesn't show the reload at all. It doesn't work vice versa, sadly, so you must be quick.

But it essentially makes it way harder for the enemy to know when you're reloading, because there is no audio cue for it, only a visual one, which is what you hide from them.

### <mark style="color:yellow;">Key Features</mark>

* <mark style="color:yellow;">Activate Gun Shield at any point during the reload animation to glitch it out.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">The glitch doesn't work the other way around.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Lets you hide the reload visually from the enemy, making it seem like you're not reloading.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Has a lot of potential in preventing the enemy from punishing you.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Lets you have</mark> [full walking speed](full-gun-shield-movement.md) <mark style="color:yellow;">during the whole reload animation.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">There is no way to stop the reload outside of disembarking or ejecting.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">You can't tell if you did it from your view.</mark>

{% hint style="info" %}
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Do the glitch quickly when the enemy is not looking (enemy in phase, blocked by electric smoke, behind cover, etc.).</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">You can activate the glitch when looking away to pretend to fire a quick 180-degree Power Shot.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Don't spam fire all your shots; instead, fire in various random bursts to make it seem like you still have ammo. Especially when low on ammo, shoot even more sparingly before doing the glitch.</mark>
  * <mark style="color:yellow;">It's quite common for you to stop shooting entirely for a few seconds, especially against another Legion with an active Gun Shield.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">You can even do it while pushing because it always applies the</mark> [full walking speed](full-gun-shield-movement.md) <mark style="color:yellow;">as long as you're reloading with an active Gun Shield.</mark>
{% endhint %}
