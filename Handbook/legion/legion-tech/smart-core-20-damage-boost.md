# ℹ️ Smart Core 20% Damage Boost

<mark style="color:yellow;">Quick Terminology</mark>

* <mark style="color:yellow;">CR = Close-Range</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">LR = Long-Range</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">PS = Power Shot</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">PC = Predator Cannon</mark>

Both Power Shots have no Titan damage falloff at range, but LR Power Shot has damage fall off on explosion, which isn't shown here.
