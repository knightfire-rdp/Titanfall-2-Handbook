# 🟢 Retrievable Gun Shield

Disembarking during the Gun Shield being deployed allows you to either retrieve a partial cooldown, an entire cooldown, or completely reset the cooldown to 0 and have to wait for it to recharge fully. \
\
Disembarking at roughly 50% Gun Shield duration left seems to be the sweet spot; later than that seems to retrieve it partially, and earlier than that causes it to restart cooldown from 0. It's always better to do it later than earlier when possible.&#x20;

### <mark style="color:yellow;">Info About Disembarking at Different Percentages</mark>

* <mark style="color:yellow;">100-50% left = Gun Shield restarts the cooldown from scratch.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">50-40% left = Gun Shield cooldown gets refreshed instantly.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">40-0% left = Gun Shield gets partially retrieved, with the amount decreasing per less % remaining.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Even if the Gun Shield gets destroyed, the glitch will still work since duration isn't tied to Gun Shield durability.</mark>
