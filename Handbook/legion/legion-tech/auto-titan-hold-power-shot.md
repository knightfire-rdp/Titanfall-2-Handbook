# 🔰 (Auto-Titan) Hold Power Shot

:beginner:"Ability to store an extra Power Shot at the inability to shoot until you use said Power Shot."

## <mark style="color:yellow;">Hold Power Shot / Triple Power Shot</mark>

Embarking onto your Titan while holding the Power Shot key throughout the entire animation will store an additional Power Shot. (assuming your current one isn't on cooldown)

### <mark style="color:yellow;">Key Features</mark>

* <mark style="color:yellow;">Allows you to store an additional Power Shot.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Holding the “fire” key or having the gun fully zoomed in will play a fake Power Shot animation with audio.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">You can stall your Power Shot and even do a Quickdraw stalled Power Shot.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Aiming down for too long or using a gun shield will forcefully fire your Power Shot.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">You're able to unaim and run but unable to fire your gun normally, switch gun mode, melee, and disembark.</mark>
* [You can easily bug yourself out.](no-disembark-bug.md)

{% embed url="https://youtu.be/KtvksBl6vpg" %}

{% hint style="info" %}
You can use the fake Power Shot animation to manipulate your opponents into wasting their resources or time. For example, playing the animation to make a Ronin phase out without actually wasting a Power Shot. It's also super useful for fighting an Ion.
{% endhint %}



## <mark style="color:yellow;">Auto-Titan Hold Power Shot /</mark> <mark style="color:yellow;">Auto-Titan Triple Power Shot</mark>

An Auto-Titan Hold Power Shot is basically identical to a Hold Power Shot except for the fact that you can also use melee. Simply embark an Auto-Legion mid-Power Shot animation.

### <mark style="color:yellow;">Key Features</mark>

* <mark style="color:yellow;">Requires an Assault Chip kit.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">You need to embark your Auto-Titan mid-Power Shot animation.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Can do everything just like the normal Hold Power Shot, but also able to use melee.</mark>
