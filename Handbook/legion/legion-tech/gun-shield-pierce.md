# 🔻 Gun Shield Pierce

Gun Shield is no stranger to piercing; similar to [Flame Shield](../../scorch/scorch-tech/thermal-shield-pierce.md) and [Vortex Shield](../../ion/ion-tech/vortex-shield-pierce.md), it can be pierced. And similar to Vortex Shield, Gun Shield is, in fact, visually a lot bigger than it actually is, well, at least towards hitscan[^1]-based weapons.&#x20;

### <mark style="color:yellow;">List of All the Ways to Pierce Gun Shield</mark>

* <mark style="color:yellow;">Melee.</mark>
* [Thermite droplets from the T-203 Thermite Launcher.](../../scorch/scorch-tech/thermite-droplets-pierce-shield.md)
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Explosive damage.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Splash damage from a long-range Power Shot.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Electric smoke.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Arc Wave fired directly at the Gun Shield itself.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Aiming at the sides of the Gun Shield with Gun Shield, up to 20%</mark>&#x20;

Surprisingly, it's visually accurate for projectiles only, meaning things that take time to reach their target can't pierce Gun Shield but are visually accurately blocked by it.

**So based on testing, the Gun Shield is 20% smaller vs. hitscan weapons but has an accurate size against projectiles.**

“Standing Pierce Video”

Legion can counter this semi-reliably, since he must angle the Gun Shield toward the target and crouch for the best block chance, but that makes him very vulnerable from the side. Crouching and angling can fully stop projectiles and 40 mm Tracker Cannon splash hits.

“Crouching Pierce Video”

{% hint style="info" %}
You can use the feet hitting feature to your advantage, you could try aiming for specific parts of the foot with an [Energy Eiphon](../../monarch/monarch-tech/double-hit-energy-siphon.md) or Laser Shot to go for a double-hit.
{% endhint %}

[^1]: Weapons that fire bullets that instantly hit the enemy from any range, like, for example, the XO-16, Predator Cannon, SMGs, and ARs.
