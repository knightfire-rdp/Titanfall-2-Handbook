# 🟢 Stalled/Quickdraw Power Shot

## <mark style="color:yellow;">Stalled Power Shot</mark>

With an unspooled gun, if you press the “Power Shot” and “fire” keys at the same time, your gun will instantly go down and play a fake Power Shot animation with sound before doing an actual Power Shot animation.

There are actually 2 different Stalled Power Shot timings; you can go from a short stall to a long stall.

Long stall is just known as “stalled Power Shot”, and the short stall is known as “stalled Quickdraw Power Shot”. Stalled Quickdraw Power Shot is as fast as normal Quickdraw Power Shot, but it's much easier to do and glows the barrel twice.

### <mark style="color:yellow;">Stalled Power Shot vs. Stalled Quickdraw Power Shot Methods</mark> <a href="#step-by-step-sequence" id="step-by-step-sequence"></a>

* <mark style="color:yellow;">Hold down the “fire” and “Power Shot” keys until your gun fully spools into position.</mark> (Left video, stalled Power Shot, slow.)
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Hold down the “fire” and “Power Shot” keys until the stalled Power Shot fires for the 2nd time.</mark> (Right video, stalled Quickdraw Power Shot, fast.)

{% embed url="https://youtu.be/VMUAufbKa1E" %}

{% hint style="info" %}
It's a neat little trick to trick a Ronin into phasing or an Ion into deploying a Vortex Shield. Since as soon as the fake Power Shot ends, the Ion/ Ronin might let go of their defensive ability and get shot for free.
{% endhint %}



## <mark style="color:yellow;">Quickdraw Power Shot</mark>

Activating Power Shot at a specific time of the gun readying up will let you power-shot faster.\
Quickdraw has a close relation to the Stalled Power Shot glitch. You can start charging a Power Shot before the Predator Cannon can fire.\
\
Quickdraw can be combined with the [Legion Melee/Sprint Quick Swap glitch](legion-melee-sprint-quick-swap.md) for an even faster Power Shot after a melee or sprinting.

{% hint style="info" %}
For a perfect Quickdraw, begin charging at the 50% mark (0.75 s) of the ADS animation. Immediate stalls, as previously mentioned, also can cause perfect Quickdraws.

Quickdraw Power Shots aren't faster than a normal Power Shot with a fully spooled Predator cannon.

Stalled or failed Quickdraw Power Shots are never slower than a normal Power Shot when unspooled.
{% endhint %}
