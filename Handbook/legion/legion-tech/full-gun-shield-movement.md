# 🟢 Full Gun Shield Movement

<mark style="color:yellow;">**When you have your Gun Shield up, you can do a reload or Mode Switch to move faster.**</mark> It makes you walk at full walking speed instead of the normal 55% (or 75% with the Light-Weight Alloy Kit). Which is roughly 82% faster, or 33% faster with the Light-Weight Alloys kit.

Useful for getting that extra movement after, for example, deploying Gun Shield to block a shot from Northstar from across the map when you spawn in. It can also be useful to move away from the enemy faster while holding the Gun Shield.&#x20;

Or even to push in a bit faster so you can, like, switch modes when trying to get around the enemy's cover faster (an example being, you push a Northstar, deploy Gun Shield to block the shot, and you switch gun modes to move faster to get closer behind her cover, since if you don't do that, Northstar will wait out the Gun Shield, shoot again, and run away).

Ofc there is some downside to it. The time you spend reloading or changing modes is the time you spend not shooting or using Power Shots, so take that into consideration. When do you want to trade the ability to attack for extra movement?

Don't mix this up with [Spartan Shield](spartan-shield.md), which gives you the ability to sprint with Gun Shield; _walking_ and _sprinting_ are 2 different things. This just lets you negate the walking speed reduction penalty when forced into aim mode because of the Gun Shield.

#### <mark style="color:yellow;">Key Features</mark>

* <mark style="color:yellow;">Reloading or switching modes with Gun Shield active lets you move at full walking speed</mark> (so roughly 82%<sup><mark style="color:orange;">**⟨1⟩**<mark style="color:orange;"></sup> faster)<mark style="color:yellow;">.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Gun Shield normally makes you walk at only 55% speed</mark> (75% with the Light-Weight Alloy kit)<mark style="color:yellow;">.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Helps in reducing time wasted in blocking distant shots that would otherwise slow you down.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Helps with pushing noticeably faster with an active Gun Shield.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">The only downside is that you need to stop shooting or power-shotting to get a speed bonus.</mark>
