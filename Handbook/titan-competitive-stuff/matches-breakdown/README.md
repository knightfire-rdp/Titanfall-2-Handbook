# 🏛️ Matches Breakdown

This is a highly experimental, fun little idea that I thought of. It revolves around making map positional analysis based on a video, like marking down all the positions and movement on the map done by anyone as best as possible. Helps to understand the match better than just rewatching the same video over and over again.

> Angelkick watched the video and drew down the positions of where the Titans possibly went during the matches following the Matches Breakdown Legend table below. Special thanks to her for doing all the graphic and video analysing work.

As of now we only have one match documented and only one page [boomtown-historical-fights.md](boomtown-historical-fights.md "mention").\
If you find those useful and want more, make sure to send [feedback.](../../welcome-to-the-titanfall-2-handbook/feedback-form.md)\
Right below is the setup of how exactly it works.



### <mark style="color:yellow;">Titan Positioning Real Time</mark>

The map is centered around the video's PoV and focuses on one Titan (the protagonist), following their whole movement around the map, involving where they look.

The enemy Titans are marked on the map too, but not as accurately, adding some element of thought and guessing as to where they were before. Makes it easier to understand the protagonist's decisions better.

Teammate positions are marked as well to help further imagine the decisions and picture what actually happened.
