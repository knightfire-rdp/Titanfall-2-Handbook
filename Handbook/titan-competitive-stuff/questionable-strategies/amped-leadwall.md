# 🧱🔫Amped Leadwall

Did you ever wish to kill a Ronin/Northstar with one mag of the Leadwall? Yes, just 4 Leadwall crit shots from up close through A-wall can enable you to have such powers.

For some unfathomable reason, Respawn decided to give amped Leadwall 110% damage increase when amped, MORE THAN DOUBLE, which is absolutely insane. For reference, a Leadwall normally deals 800 total Titan damage per shot and 1,200 damage with a crit. But once amped, it skyrockets to 1,680 total Titan damage per shot and 2,520 damage with a crit.

Moreover, <mark style="color:yellow;">it also loses all damage falloff against Titans and Pilots!</mark>

### <mark style="color:yellow;">Damage Table Normal vs. Amped</mark>

Leadwall fires 8 projectiles total per shot, so damage will be calculated in total rather than per individual projectile.

| Normal/Amped                                                                                 | Normal Damage (Far)                                                                 | Crit Damage (Far)                                                                   |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Normal vs. Titans (far)                                                                      | 800 (600)                                                                           | 1,200 (900)                                                                         |
| <mark style="color:yellow;">Amped vs. Titans</mark> <mark style="color:orange;">(far)</mark> | <mark style="color:yellow;">1,680</mark> <mark style="color:orange;">(1,680)</mark> | <mark style="color:yellow;">2,520</mark> <mark style="color:orange;">(2,520)</mark> |
| Normal vs. Pilots (far)                                                                      | 480 (200)                                                                           | N.A.                                                                                |
| <mark style="color:yellow;">Amped vs. Pilots</mark> <mark style="color:orange;">(far)</mark> | <mark style="color:yellow;">480</mark> <mark style="color:orange;">(400)</mark>     | N.A.                                                                                |

{% hint style="info" %}
As you can tell, the amped Leadwall doesn't have damage falloff, unlike regular Leadwall, so its damage stays the same, even when hitting a target at the edge of the max range.
{% endhint %}
