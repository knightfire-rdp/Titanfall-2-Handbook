# 🌫️🎯Threat Optics E-Smoke

One hell of a sus strategy that involves electric smoke (preferably with the Counter Ready kit) and the Northstar Threat Optics kit which lets you see enemies through e-smoke. Yeah, pretty self explainatory, you get to roleplay bush campers from Fortnite in Titanfall 2.

Do keep in mind that your Plasma Railgun glow will be more visible through e-smoke the higher the charge, giving the far most glow when fully charged. You can repeatly charge and uncharge right before reaching the full charge to remain hidden. Fully charge it right before firing for maximum toxicity.
