# 💥😵Softball Torture

There are actually 2 main ways to use this strategy, and they both ideally require Amped Wall to work. Those 2 methods were first seen being used by OvalBasher360 in the 4v4 LTS tournament.

## <mark style="color:yellow;">Long-Distance Battery Softball Sniping</mark>

Quite self-explanatory. Park your Titan far away from the battery, and use the softball with Amped Wall to lob grenades at a far distance over the obstacles and into the battery area to one-shot the enemy Pilot. This method requires knowing about the softball angles to hit the battery area from different angles. But with it, it's possible to supposedly hit the enemy battery from spawn on every map.

Not much is revealed about the shot line-ups yet; still a work in progress, it seems.

## <mark style="color:yellow;">Softball Mines</mark>

This is another method using the Softball, but in a different sense. Stand on a battery spawn point, aim straight down and fire the Softball. If the terrain is flat, the projectiles will stay stuck to the battery platform and won't despawn when you die or the round resets. Any enemy Pilot or Titan that touches the clumped-up Softball projectiles will take a ton of damage or outright die.

Thankfully the clumped-up Softball projectiles make a very loud noise, so it's possible to know about their whereabouts in advance. Although when the round ends or if the player who trapped the battery dies, then the softballs will lose their highly visible yellow glow, making them harder to see. You can easily dodge the trap by simply not touching the surface of the battery platform, you can grab the battery from midair.

You can also use a Gravity Star to clean up the Softball projectiles, since the moment they land on non-battery platform terrain, they'll explode shortly. Though you can also weaponize this and use it to delete Titans if done right. You can even place an Amped Wall beside the platform and fire in amped Softball projectiles for even more damage.

<mark style="color:yellow;">For reference</mark>\
<mark style="color:yellow;">An unamped softball explosion deals</mark> <mark style="color:orange;">330</mark> <mark style="color:yellow;">Titan damage and</mark> <mark style="color:orange;">90</mark> <mark style="color:yellow;">entity damage.</mark>\
(Need 38 Softball explosions to doom an Ogre class Titan, 5 bars of damage.)

<mark style="color:yellow;">When amped it's explosion deals</mark> <mark style="color:orange;">530</mark> <mark style="color:yellow;">Titan damage and</mark> <mark style="color:orange;">150</mark> <mark style="color:yellow;">entity damage.</mark>\
(Need 24 amped Softball projectiles to doom an Ogre class Titan, 5 bars of damage, for reference Ion can hold up to 31 projectiles in her Vortex Shield at once…)

### <mark style="color:yellow;">Key Features</mark>

* Ability to trap battery platforms in Last Titan Standing mode.
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Softball projectiles stay stuck to the platform if fired directly straight down at it.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">The projectiles will persist upon user death or round ending, they'll even lose their glow.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">If an enemy Pilot or Titan comes in contact with the projectiles, they'll explode.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Softball projectiles don't explode upon being destroyed like Satchels, so a Pilot won't waste all of them at once.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Can use a Gravity Star to propel forth a ton of Softball projectiles at once, shredding Titans.</mark>
