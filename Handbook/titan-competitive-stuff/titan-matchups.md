# ⚔️ Titan Matchups

## <mark style="color:yellow;">Strict 1v1 Matchups</mark>

> Special thanks to Justaperson for writing out all of the matchup information and explaining them all to me inperson.

Only considering comp at the highest level and against 1 opponent, under LTS rules, which assume that both parties will play the objective (batteries) to win.\
They'll be rated on a scale from 1 to 9.\
The fight is set to be won.

#### <mark style="color:yellow;">Matchup Level Rating</mark>

1 - Virtually impossible\
2 - Usually insurmountable disadvantage\
3 - Substantial disadvantage\
4 - Manageable disadvantage\
5 - Only marginal advantages/disadvantages\
6 - Noticeable advantage\
7 - Significant advantage\
8 - Usually assured victory\
9 - Carelessness afforded\
\
Example: Scorch card with "Northstar 1" written on it reads as\
“When Scorch fights Northstar, it is virtually impossible to win at the highest level.”
