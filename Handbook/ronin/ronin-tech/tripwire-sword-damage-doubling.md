# 🟢 Tripwire Sword Damage Doubling

Hitting a Titan/Titans and a Tripwire in one swing will cause your sword to deal double damage.\
The Tripwire doesn't really count as an entity, so when you hit both the Tripwire and the Titan, your melee will hit the Titan twice.

### <mark style="color:yellow;">**Key Features**</mark>&#x20;

* <mark style="color:yellow;">Hitting multiple pylons at once doesn't increase damage.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Multiple Tripwires will NOT increase the damage.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Aiming for the Tripwire is recommended; you need to hit the Tripwire before the Titan/Titans.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Can affect multiple Titans at once.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Damage won't spill over to doomstate if the enemy is not doomed yet.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Normal melee</mark> (800 ⇒ 1,600) <mark style="color:yellow;">and Sword Core melee</mark> (2,200 ⇒ 4,400) <mark style="color:yellow;">total damage per swing doubled.</mark>
