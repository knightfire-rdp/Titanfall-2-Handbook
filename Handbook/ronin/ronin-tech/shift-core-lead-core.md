# 🔰 Shift Core / Lead Core

:beginner:”Ability to have Leadwall in Sword Core at the cost of a single mistake ruining your whole core, making you outright defenseless, and also removing crouch tech for [Shotblock](shotblock.md) during it.”

## <mark style="color:yellow;">Introduction</mark>

Pressing the “primary weapon switch key” (“1” by default) after blocking or deploying an Arc Wave unlocks your Leadwall. Though it comes at a cost and risks too…

### <mark style="color:yellow;">**Key Features**</mark>&#x20;

* <mark style="color:yellow;">Ability to unlock your gun.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Able to switch back to Sword Core melee and vice versa at will.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Can block and deploy Arc Wave while the gun is unlocked.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">A special melee animation for switching back to the sword for meleeing can be sped up.</mark>
* <mark style="color:red;">Can bug out the Sword Core entirely.</mark>
* <mark style="color:red;">When sword-blocking/arc-waving, you get the slowwall bug.</mark>

## <mark style="color:yellow;">Gun Mode / Leadwall Unlock /</mark> <mark style="color:yellow;">Lead Core</mark>

It is very situational considering the stupidly high DPS that the Sword Core melees offer; basically, you're trading in high DPS for a longer-reaching weapon that can't be melee pinned.

It seems most useful when fighting Scorch from a distance to avoid Flame Shield and floor thermite damage or just to fight Titans safely in general while doomed to avoid getting executed.

Due to how many buttons there are to press, moreover [Quickswap](../../universal-mechanics/quickswap-crouch-tech.md) being able to be used, and never forgetting to press “1” when sword-blocking/arc-waving, this glitch becomes a skill challenging to master.

## <mark style="color:yellow;">Switching Back to Melee</mark>

Switching back to melee during gun mode plays a special long animation after pressing the melee key once. You'll automatically melee after the animation finishes, with a delay in between, hence why it's a lot more ideal to skip a large portion of the animation via Quickswap Sword Block or Arc Wave.

## <mark style="color:yellow;">The Cost and the Risk: Slowwall and Stripped Core Bug</mark>

<mark style="color:yellow;">Slowwall</mark> is the name for the bug that causes unlocked Leadwall to have a very significant delay when shotblocking or arc-waving, basically nerfing your ability to shotblock too fast, or your gun won't fire at all. It'll always be active during Dash Core. Notice how I was able to shotblock all 4 shots before I even got to fire the third slowwall shot, despite the last 2 shots being spam fired because it was already behind.

<mark style="color:yellow;">Stripped Core</mark> is basically the bug that causes your sword and Leadwall to fully unequip, stripping you out of any abilities/weapons except for phase. The bug happens when you forget to press “1” after sword-blocking/arc-waving during Shift Core. It can be fixed only after the core ends by pressing “1” once.
