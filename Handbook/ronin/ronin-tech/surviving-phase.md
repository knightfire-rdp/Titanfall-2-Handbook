# 🟢 Surviving Phase

Phasing into a Titan will instakill you unless you hold Sword Block. Holding Sword Block while phasing inside a Titan will deal up to a bar of damage; if you're lucky, you could [phase bounce](phase-movement-tech.md#phase-bounce) and take 0 damage even without blocking.

From testing, crouching does NOT affect the chance to kill Ronin through phase.
