# 🟢 Shotblock

Shotblock is a tech term that means using [Quickswap](../../universal-mechanics/quickswap-crouch-tech.md) to instantly block after firing your Leadwall. Known and used by all Ronin mains in the comp scene, as it lets them fire Leadwall and Sword Block before the enemy can even punish. It's not punishable on reaction, even against a Northstar with a charged shot waiting for an opening to shoot through. &#x20;
