# 🔻 Sword Pierce

## <mark style="color:yellow;">Sword Pierce Types</mark>

Sword Pierce is an intended game feature that was not finished nor scrapped by Respawn. It was left in game files, and it basically lets you pierce sword block in different spots and angles. It's very consistent with most weapons, mainly hitscan bullets.&#x20;

There are 2 main ways to pierce sword lock, named them “soft pierce” and “hard pierce”. Soft pierce refers to spots that can be pierced by certain weapons, so, somewhat consistently, about 50-90% depending on the weapon and spot. Hard spots are difficult-to-reach spots that offer 100% pierce consistency to all weapons; heck, even explosions.

You can tell it's sword pierce when you see a hitmarker on your shot. This page is based of [#second-part-includes-sword-block-and-defensive-kits](../../universal-mechanics/titan-ability-interactions.md#second-part-includes-sword-block-and-defensive-kits "mention").

## <mark style="color:yellow;">Soft Pierce</mark>

Refers to spots that can be pierced by certain weapons, so, somewhat consistently, about 40-90% depending on the weapon and spot. Ofc there are some exceptions, like the Cluster Missile that pierces like once or twice during the whole explosion animation.&#x20;

It seems to be straight behind the Ronin, with up to 80 degrees to his right. The left side can't be pierced at all. There's a second soft pierce spot on the underside of the right heel. The heel spot is a soft pierce but will be a hard pierce spot if the Ronin is on elevation or you're using satchels.&#x20;

### <mark style="color:yellow;">Soft Pierce Damage Output</mark> (Approximate)

* <mark style="color:yellow;">Splitter Rifle = 40% more damage.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Laser Core = 70% more damage.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">T-203 Thermite Launcher = like 90% more damage.</mark> (If Ronin's right heel is touching the droplet.)
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Flame Shield = 70% more damage.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Flame Trap = 5%?</mark> (Saw like only 2 hitmarkers when Ronin around in the thermite, similar to Cluster Missile in terms of performance; setting up the canister higher doesn't help.)
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Firewall = 30% more damage.</mark> (This abomination WORKS ONLY if the Ronin walks along the path of the Firewall, from the start point towards the end point while looking in that direction; otherwise, it'll never pierce.)
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Cluster Missile = 5%?</mark> (Saw only 1-2 hitmarkers.)
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Rocket Barrage = 10-50% more damage.</mark> (Requires a specific angle for 80-95% more damage.)
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Salvo Core = 90% more damage.</mark> (Fairly easy to do with airborne core.)
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Predator Cannon and XO-16 = 40-50% more damage.</mark> (Due to long-range mode having less spread than XO-16, it's a lot easier to get 50% on long-range mode.)
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Smart Core =</mark> <mark style="color:yellow;">30-40% more damage.</mark> (Aims for body center.)
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Rocket Salvo = 10-20% more damage.</mark>

### <mark style="color:red;">Can't Soft Pierce</mark>

* <mark style="color:red;">Laser Shot, Tripwire.</mark>
* <mark style="color:red;">Flame Core.</mark>
* <mark style="color:red;">Railgun, Flight Core.</mark>
* <mark style="color:red;">40 mm Cannon</mark>
* <mark style="color:red;">Close-range and long-range Power Shot</mark>
* <mark style="color:red;">Energy Siphon.</mark> (Referring to damage, not shield drain.)
* <mark style="color:red;">Pilot weapons: SMR, EPG, Cold War, charge rifle, MGL, etc.</mark>

## <mark style="color:yellow;">Hard Pierce</mark>

Well, aside from melee damage, you can pierce Ronin for 100% consistency in 4 ways. Either being underneath the Ronin and shooting from below him. Piercing his right foot with wave attacks on a chest-height obstacle requires Ronin in a special block animation. Piercing his right foot on the ground with a satchel.&#x20;

It works on all weapons, but yeah, it depends on the spot. &#x20;

### <mark style="color:yellow;">The Four Hard Pierce Methods</mark>

* <mark style="color:yellow;">From beneath Ronin.</mark> (Free range to shoot anywhere so long as you're directly below him.)
* <mark style="color:yellow;">From underneath Ronin's left arm during special block animation.</mark> (Requires you to be close, can even hit the crit spot.)
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Wave attacks can pierce both feet on chest-height obstacles if he's in his special block animation.</mark> (If you actually manage it in an actual game with Flame Core, message me the video.)
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Satchels being the only explosive able to pierce the right foot.</mark> (Up to 5 meters in front of and near it.)&#x20;

### <mark style="color:yellow;">From Beneath Ronin Hard Pierce</mark>

Strangely enough, it's not just about shooting below the feet but just being at the angle below the Ronin. You can hit him anywhere from below, even his face and weak spots.&#x20;

### <mark style="color:yellow;">Underneath Ronin's Left Arm Hard Pierce</mark>

Ronin changes his block animation to 3 different types when getting damaged while blocking. Here is the screenshot of the animation that is required for the sword pierce from below his left arm to be possible. He cycles through those animations randomly, and it's not dependent on where you hurt/shoot him; it's random.&#x20;

Ronin can ofc just unblock and block again to revert the Sword Block animation to a different one. But this lets you pierce him with anything up close.

### <mark style="color:yellow;">Satchel Right Foot Hard Pierce</mark>

Yeah, so apparently Respawn created a patch to prevent explosive projectiles from piercing Sword Block when aimed at the feet, like long-range Power Shot. Furthermore, not even frag grenades can pierce the feet from above. But satchels can, so it seems like satchels are a form of entity that isn't an actual projectile and so managed to evade the patch.

At least that's my theory, but basically, you can very consistently sword-pierce Ronin's right foot with satchels, whether behind, on the foot, or heck, even 5 meters in front, it'll pierce easily.&#x20;
