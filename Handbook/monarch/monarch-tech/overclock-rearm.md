# 🟢 Overclock Rearm

Overclock Rearm is an interesting tech that lets you activate Rearm earlier after using an Energy Siphon or Rocket Salvo and to make Rearm animation faster. Normally there is a decent delay after using one of those abilities where you need to wait before you can fire the Chaingun or fire an ability, but Rearm on the other hand is unaffected. Even better, it's stronger when done right after one of those abilities.

The delay removal is big, while the speed increase on the Rearm animation itself feels like 20-30% faster, and it even works with Rearm and Reload Upgrade Core. It works with all Upgrade Core ability upgrades and even with the “[Single-Shot Rocket Fire](single-shot-rocket-fire.md)” tech.&#x20;

Why the term “Overclock Rearm”? Well, because it means to push a system beyond normal operating speed, earlier ability start, faster than normal animation speed, and lets you cycle abilities faster overall. So it quite literally feels like you're “overclocking” Monarch's systems.

### <mark style="color:yellow;">**Step-by-Step Sequence**</mark>

1. <mark style="color:yellow;">Activate Energy Siphon / Rocket Salvo or other variants.</mark>
2. <mark style="color:yellow;">Press and hold the Rearm key the moment the ability fires.</mark>
3. <mark style="color:yellow;">Hold until you see Rearm animation start.</mark>
