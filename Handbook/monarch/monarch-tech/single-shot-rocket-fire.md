# 🟢 Single-Shot Rocket Fire

By pressing Rearm just after activating Rocket Salvo, you'll cancel the Rocket Salvo mid-animation yet still fire a singular, or in rare cases, 2 rockets. Surprisingly enough, it'll consume a bit of your ability, and you'll need to wait for it to recharge. You can't fire the Rocket Salvo until the tiny bit of duration regenerates in, like, a few seconds. Unless you're running MTMS[^1], then you can fire again before waiting.

&#x20;This tech works on all core upgrades too. You do need Rearm to perform this glitch. If you try to activate Rearm too late, it won't work at all, and you'll continue firing the rocket salvo like normal; it has a small interruption window. It also doesn't work with melee, but you can melee cancel Rearm to prevent the Rearm animation from finishing; you just need to interrupt the Rocket Salvo, nothing else.

&#x20;What use case does it have? Well, aside from a potential one rocket Pilot goose action for some fancy montage, it can probably be used to destroy Tether Traps without using all of your rockets or to trick/confuse enemy players.

### <mark style="color:yellow;">**Step-by-Step Sequence**</mark>

1. <mark style="color:yellow;">Have all your abilities refreshed.</mark>
2. <mark style="color:yellow;">Activate Rocket Salvo.</mark>
3. <mark style="color:yellow;">Nearly instantly after, activate Rearm.</mark>
   1. <mark style="color:yellow;">Optionally melee right after to cancel the Rearm itself to not use it.</mark>
