# 🟢 One Bar Monarch Glitch

You can glitch your Monarch's health into a singular bar that you won't notice inside the Titan, but only outside the Titan. You must not be doomed for this to work; the less HP, the easier it is to do. Furthermore, you basically want to self-damage yourself during embark with Rocket Salvo to doom yourself just before the battery registers as you embark. If you're too slow, you won't get the glitch to work.

### <mark style="color:yellow;">**Key Features**</mark>

* <mark style="color:yellow;">Your whole HP will convert into one bar until you get doomed and pull a new battery.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">You can pull batteries while not doomed without breaking the glitch.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">That one bar technically counts as 100% of your HP, so you can win an LTS match via health advantage.</mark>
