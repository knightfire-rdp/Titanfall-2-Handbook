# 🔻 Accelerator Acceleration Bug

There's a minor bug involving Accelerator Core Upgrade, which happens when activating the Accelerator mid-firing. Causing your Chaingun to fire normally, but with the Accelerator sound effect. Thankfully, there are easy ways to fix that bug. &#x20;

### <mark style="color:yellow;">Ways to fix the bug</mark>

* <mark style="color:yellow;">Stop firing briefly before firing again.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Reload and then firing.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Activating Accelerator Core Upgrade before firing or during reload.</mark>
