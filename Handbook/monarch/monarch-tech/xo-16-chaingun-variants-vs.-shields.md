# ℹ️ XO-16 Chaingun Variants vs. Shields

Monarch's XO-16 Chaingun can get buffed by 2 Upgrade Core upgrades, namely the Arc Rounds and Accelerator. When combined, they shred the shields of other Titans, mainly due to Arc Rounds that drain enemy shield abilities similar to core abilities.\
This page is a more in-depth version of [titan-ability-interactions.md](../../universal-mechanics/titan-ability-interactions.md "mention") page, but only centered around Monarch's XO-16 Chaingun.

<mark style="color:yellow;">**Arc Rounds deal roughly 50% extra damage to overshield, Particle Wall, Gun Shield, Amped Wall, and Hard Cover. They also cause roughly 3% drain effect per shot to Vortex, Thermal, and Inferno Shield.**</mark>

### <mark style="color:yellow;">For Reference</mark>

<mark style="color:yellow;">Terms</mark>\
<mark style="color:yellow;">XO16</mark> = normal XO-16 bullets\
<mark style="color:yellow;">Arc</mark> = Arc Rounds bullets\
<mark style="color:yellow;">Accel</mark> = Accelerator + Arc Rounds bullets\
<mark style="color:yellow;">Mag</mark> = ammo magazine\
<mark style="color:blue;">Arc damage = Additional damage from arc rounds that only affects shields.</mark>



<mark style="color:yellow;">Entity Health</mark>\
Particle Wall = 2,000 HP\
Reinforced Particle Wall = 3,000 HP\
Gun Shield = 2,500 HP\
Bulwark = 5,000 HP\
Amped Wall = 1000 HP\
Hard Cover = 850 HP<br>

<mark style="color:yellow;">Entity Damage per Bullet</mark>\
XO-16 = <mark style="color:yellow;">45</mark> damage  \[DPS: 540]\
Arc Round = <mark style="color:yellow;">45</mark> damage + <mark style="color:blue;">23</mark> arc damage <mark style="color:blue;">(68 total shield damage)</mark> <mark style="color:blue;">\[DPS: 816]</mark>\
Accelerator Arc Round = <mark style="color:yellow;">56</mark> damage + <mark style="color:blue;">28</mark> arc damage <mark style="color:blue;">(84 total shield damage)</mark> <mark style="color:blue;">\[DPS: 672 -> 1,512]</mark>



<mark style="color:yellow;">Titan Health</mark>\
Full Overshield = 2,500 HP<br>

<mark style="color:yellow;">Titan Damage per Bullet</mark>\
XO-16 = <mark style="color:yellow;">120</mark> damage \[DPS: 1,440]\
Arc Round = <mark style="color:yellow;">120</mark> damage + <mark style="color:blue;">60</mark> arc damage <mark style="color:blue;">(180 total shield damage)</mark> <mark style="color:blue;">\[DPS: 2,160]</mark>\
Accelerator Arc Round = <mark style="color:yellow;">150</mark> damage + <mark style="color:blue;">75</mark> arc damage <mark style="color:blue;">(225 total shield damage)</mark> <mark style="color:blue;">\[DPS: 1,800 -> 4,050]</mark>

It's only significantly different if you ramp up the Accelerator with Arc Rounds before aiming at a shield.</mark>
