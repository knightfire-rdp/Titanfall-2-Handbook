# 🌟 Perfect Solo Insane Rise

## <mark style="color:yellow;">Introduction & Video</mark>

A walkthrough on how to do Perfect Solo Insane Frontier Defense on Rise. Basically FD on Rise done on Insane difficulty, without any Titan and Pilot deaths, and done solo.

> A walkthrough written by Dinosaurd123.

Perfect solo insane routing using my (Dinosaurd123) 27:46 run for reference.

{% embed url="https://youtu.be/EjswtveGdQ8" %}

{% hint style="info" %}
Important note for wave 1: Damage as many Stalkers as possible, even if unable to kill them before they reach the Harvester. If they blow up without being damaged, you will not receive kill credit/money.
{% endhint %}



### <mark style="color:yellow;">Wave 1 Start</mark>

1. Begin the wave at the back of the map. Instakill Nuke Titan (2 amped Satchels, 2 amped Archers, rodeo). Plant 2 Satchels at Reaper spawn to instakill the Reaper that spawns around 1:09.
2. Rotate back to the Harvester to intercept Grunts/Stalkers that reach the Harvester around 1:12.
3. Let the 2nd Nuke Titan blow up on the Harvester to conserve Archer ammunition. Roughly 50 enemies remaining around 3:00 if on proper pace.
4. With around 40-50 enemies remaining, pre-place 2 amped Satchels in the center lane to deal with the double Nuke Titans push that'll be arriving soon. Kill at least one Nuke Titan using a combination of 5 amped Archers/Satchels (at least one must die to preserve Harvester health, but both can be killed with properly used Satchels).
5. Use the remaining 4 amped Archer rounds and 2 Satchels to kill the remaining Reaper and Nuke Titan to finish the round.

### <mark style="color:yellow;">End of Wave 1 Intermission</mark>

<mark style="color:yellow;">Budget: Roughly $2035.</mark> (Assuming you damaged most of the Stalkers.)

* Buy 1 arc trap.
* Place 1 arc trap in the center of the map intersection. Place 2 Satchels on the center bridge to instakill the first Reaper that jumps up there.



### <mark style="color:yellow;">Wave 2 Start</mark>

1. Kill the Stalkers/Grunts that spawn left of the Harvester, followed by the right side of the Harvester (there's a chance some of these despawn).
2. After killing at least the Grunts, rush to the center intersection and hold the line there throughout the wave. If done properly, the whole wave can be cleared without doubling back to the Harvester.
3. If enemies do make it past the center intersection, delay doubling back to deal with them for as long as possible to hopefully clear out all the remaining spawns first.

### <mark style="color:yellow;">End of Wave 2 intermission</mark>

<mark style="color:yellow;">Budget: Roughly $3505.</mark>

* Buy 2 sentry turrets.
* Place them at the left side of the building in front of the Harvester to form a sentry blockade, as seen in the pictures.

### <mark style="color:yellow;">Wave 3 Start</mark>

1. Begin the wave at the left-side zone along the midpoint of the map. Kill both Arc Ronins then rotate to the center to deal with the 3rd Arc Titan (if you only have time to kill one, then prioritize the Arc Titan on the right side). If you missed either the far-left or center lane's Arc Ronin then rotate back to the Harvester to finish them off.
2. Rotate to the back of the map in the center lane while killing as many Stalkers along the way as you can.
3. Kill both Reapers and the Arc Ronin in the back of the center lane, then fall back to the middle of the map. Kill the remaining 3 Reapers on the left side and the last Reaper in the center lane.
4. Once the Reapers are dead, rotate back to the Harvester and alternate between killing the enemies in the center lane and the right side as needed. Be sure to kill the 8 Plasma Drones as quickly as you can.
5. At around 10 enemies remaining, you should have a Tone and an Arc Ronin push coming from the right side lane to finish the wave.
6. If not doomed, you can farm batteries from the sniper Tone on the right side to farm back to full health without buying amped batteries to save money.

### <mark style="color:yellow;">End of wave 3 intermission</mark>

<mark style="color:yellow;">Budget: Roughly $4130.</mark>

* Buy 3 Harvester boosts.
* Place 2 Satchels on the left lane bridge to instakill the first Reaper that jumps onto there.



### <mark style="color:yellow;">Wave 4 Start</mark>

1. Begin at the Harvester intersection to kill the first 28 Plasma Drones. As soon as the 28 Plasma Drones are dead, rotate to the center bridge to intersect the Arc Ronins, Scorches, and Reapers.
2. Kill as many Arc-Ronins/Scorches/Reapers as you can, then rotate to the harvester with around 45 enemies remaining to fight the Titans, Reapers, and Plasma Drones that are stacking up in the center lane near the Harvester.
3. At around 10 enemies remaining, you'll get a large Arc Ronin push coming from the center and left-side lanes to finish the round.
4. If not doomed, you can farm batteries from the sniper Tone on the left side to farm back to full health without buying amped batteries to save money.

### <mark style="color:yellow;">End of wave 4 intermission</mark>

<mark style="color:yellow;">Budget: Roughly $3270.</mark>

* Buy 2 Harvester boosts.
* With the remaining funds, buy either amped batteries or an extra arc trap.



### <mark style="color:yellow;">Wave 5 Start & Finale</mark>

1. Begin at the back of the map in the center lane. Instantly kill the Tone and Arc Ronin that attempt to go through the right side, then rush back to the Harvester to deal with the Plasma Drones and the Titans that are coming from the left side lane.
2. Remain at the Harvester center intersection until all Plasma Drones have been cleared for the wave. Harvester boost through Nuke Titan explosions as needed until the Plasma Drones have been dealt with.
3. Once all Plasma Drones are dead (roughly 48 enemies remaining), rotate up the right side to deal with the oncoming Nuke Titans.
4. At roughly 43 enemies remaining, temporarily abandon the right side to kill the 2 Nuke Titans that are approaching the Harvester from the center lane, then return to the right side.
5. Around 36 enemies remain, and you'll start seeing Arc Ronins mixed in on the right side as well.
6. Once the remaining Nuke Titans and **3 Arc Ronins** have been killed on the right side, rotate to the Harvester center intersection to deal with the Nuke Titans coming from the left and center lanes.
7. At around 20 enemies remaining, you'll get an Arc Ronin and some Nuke Titans in each of the 3 lanes at the same time. Following this, you'll have several additional Nukes on the right side.
8. At around 11 enemies remaining, you'll get a final onslaught of&#x20;
   1. Arc Ronins and Scorches in the left lane.
   2. Arc Ronins and Scorches in the center lane.
   3.  Arc Ronins and Nukes in the right lane to finish out the wave.
