# 🗺️ Handbook Legend

The emotes have specific meanings; here are the translations for relevant page emotes (the unlisted emotes are merely for decoration):

## <mark style="color:yellow;">Legend</mark>

:information\_source:⁣= Information or tech related solely to movement.

:biohazard: = Not fully understood.

:green\_circle: = Tech that benefits the Titan.

:beginner: = Tradeoff Tech that mainly benefits the Titan with some kind of small downside to it. Disembark requirement does not count as Tradeoff Tech!

* (Partial green to signal the good part and partial yellow to signal the slight bad side.)

:small\_red\_triangle\_down: = Tech that hinders/harms the Titan when used or used against it.





## <mark style="color:yellow;">Damage Values Explained for Spreadsheets</mark>

This is very important to know for understanding the numerous damage spreadsheets mentioned around in the handbook.\
Each weapon, ability, and even core uses 2 different damage types. \
They exist mainly to prevent Titans from instantly oblirating Pilots and solid shields.

<mark style="color:yellow;">Titan Damage</mark> tells you how much damage it does to Titan class entities.\
Those being:

* <mark style="color:yellow;">Titan and Overshield.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Reapers and Dropships.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Tripwire pylons.</mark>

<mark style="color:yellow;">Entity Damage:</mark> How much damage it does to small entities and solid shield based entities.\
Those being:

* <mark style="color:yellow;">Pilots.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Grunts, Spectres, Stalkers, Plasma Drones, Sentries.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Solid shields, specifically Particle Wall, Gun Shield, Amped Wall, Hard Cover.</mark>
  * (Arc Wave & Flame Core deal Titan damage against Particle Wall & Gun Shield.)
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Tether Traps and Satchels.</mark>





## <mark style="color:yellow;">Vocabulary</mark>

Abilities and Cores have capitalized names according to Titanfall 2, but I will turn them into slang verbs for the sake of avoiding repetitive wording. For example: <mark style="color:yellow;">“Firing a Power Shot.” → “Is power-shotting a Titan”</mark>

&#x20;<mark style="color:yellow;">“ADS”</mark> stands for “aim down sights,” so aiming down a weapon.

<mark style="color:yellow;">“Awall/A-Wall”</mark> stands for “amped wall.”

<mark style="color:yellow;">“Crit”</mark> is popular slang within video games for “critical,” aka “critical hit.” Which refers to an attack that deals, often, double the damage through chance or when shooting a weak spot.  &#x20;

<mark style="color:yellow;">“Esmoke/E-smoke”</mark> stands for “electric smoke.”

<mark style="color:yellow;">“Hitscan”</mark> is literally anything that isn't a projectile, instant and not visible in the air, so most bullets and lasers.

<mark style="color:yellow;">“LTS”</mark> stands for “Last Titan Standing.”

<mark style="color:yellow;">“Pugs”</mark> does not refer to the dog but instead stands for “pickup games,” basically competitive private matches for Titanfall 2.

<mark style="color:yellow;">“Tech”</mark> is short for “technology” but implies a glitch/feature that benefits/harms the Titan.
