# 🔰 Energy Refill/Waste from Executions

:beginner: ⁣”Ability to regen a decent amount of energy from an execution, but also the ability to end up with less energy afterward.”

It's a strange thing that happens during both executions, where Ion will fire a Laser Shot. If you have at least 55% energy (enough to fire a Laser Shot), you'll spend that energy to fire said Laser Shot and end up with a lot less energy.

But on the other side, if you execute someone with like 40% or less energy, you'll still fire the Laser Shot, but free of cost. Similar to the [ZPL glitch.](zero-point-laser-shot-zpl.md) This in turn will let you regenerate energy throughout that whole execution and end up with a bonus of like 30-40% free energy.&#x20;

So it's recommended to have less than 40% energy when executing; use it up or something. Having more than 40% is tricky. Even if you laser-shot at full energy right before execution, you'll be at like 45% energy, but the execution will be long enough that you'll regen enough energy to use the Laser Shot normally. That free energy regain is very useful, so it comes with risk and reward.

#### <mark style="color:yellow;">Key Features</mark>

* <mark style="color:yellow;">Both executions work.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Ion execution Laser Shot is real and will consume your energy if there is enough.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">If there is not enough energy, the Ion will do ZPL and you'll end up with more energy after.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Recommended to execute at like 40% or less energy to regain energy during execution.</mark>
