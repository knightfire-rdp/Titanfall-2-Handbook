# 🔰 Rage Quit Laser Core

:beginner:”Ability to get a Laser Core from an execution, but you'll get your core gain reset.”

Basically, if an opponent leaves during the Ion Prime execution, you'll start firing a “free” Laser Core.

Ofc it's not as free as you may think; instead, it'll drain all the progress you've made towards earning your Laser Core. So if that happens while having 90% core saved up. You'll be reset back to 0% (or 20% with Overcore).

Other than trusting the opponent to not ragequit mid-core or changing execution style to normal execution, there is no way to prevent it.
