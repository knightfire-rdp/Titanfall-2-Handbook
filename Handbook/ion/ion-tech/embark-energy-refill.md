# 🟢 Embark Energy Refill

## <mark style="color:yellow;">Embark Energy Refill</mark>

Disembarking and re-embarking will instantly fill energy, and, like every other Titan, you can reload your primary weapon instantly. Works best with the Phase Embark kit.

* <mark style="color:yellow;">Instantly refill energy.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Instantly reload Splitter Rifle ammo.</mark>

{% embed url="https://youtu.be/NlsSi0nT1gw" %}

{% hint style="info" %}
Useful when going for a battery or to do something quickly as a Pilot, like place Satchel landmines. It can also be used on maps like Black Water Canal to out damage a Northstar by constantly laser-shotting back.&#x20;
{% endhint %}
