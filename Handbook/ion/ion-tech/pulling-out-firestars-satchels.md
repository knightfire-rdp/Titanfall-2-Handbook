# 🟢 Pulling out Firestars/Satchels

There are 2 different versions of this tech: one is for pulling out Firestars/satchels from your Titan, and the other one is for pulling them out of your teammates' Titans.

## <mark style="color:yellow;">**Self-Pull**</mark>

Vortex has such a huge hitbox that you can take advantage of. If a Firestar/satchel gets stuck on your Titan anywhere, just open your Vortex Shield and flick your screen sharply around to grab it.



## <mark style="color:yellow;">**Teammate Pull**</mark>

Pulling Firestars/satchels out of teammates is a lot easier; just get close to them and aim your Vortex Shield at the Firestar/satchel and pull it out.
