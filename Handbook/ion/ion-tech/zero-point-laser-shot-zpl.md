# 🟢 Zero Point Laser Shot (ZPL)

There's a problem with the game's ability to deal with missing energy while you're charging a laser Shot.

### <mark style="color:yellow;">Key Features</mark>

* <mark style="color:yellow;">Ion's energy pool caps at 100% energy.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Laser Shot firing costs 55% energy.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Tripwire deployment costs 35% energy.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">ZPL makes no audio for enemies to detect.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">ZPL helps fire faster than normal Laser Shot, allowing less delay before vortex-shielding.</mark>

With this in mind, if you have roughly 55% energy, enough to fire only the Laser Shot, hold the Laser Shot and deploy a Tripwire before firing. The game will be confused and will let you fire the Laser Shot for free. Sure, you have to use 35% energy for a Tripwire to activate, but it's a superb trade to have a Laser Shot and a Tripwire for only 35% energy!
