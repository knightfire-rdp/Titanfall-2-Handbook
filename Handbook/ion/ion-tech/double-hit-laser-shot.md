# ☣️ Double-Hit Laser Shot

Hitting a specific enemy Titan model spot while the Titan is moving will cause the Laser Shot to deal damage twice. The running theory is that Respawn didn't fully fit the stationary hitbox inside the moving one. Basically, Titans have 2 animations, one for moving and one for standing still. Laser Shot by default has splash, so it probably hits the moving hitbox and then edge splashes into an exposed part of the stationary hitbox.

### <mark style="color:yellow;">**Known Info about this Glitch**</mark>

* <mark style="color:yellow;">Affects Laser Shot and</mark> [Energy Siphon](../../monarch/monarch-tech/double-hit-energy-siphon.md) <mark style="color:yellow;">alike.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Laser Shot deals damage twice.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Each Titan chassis has a slightly different spot, Prime versions too.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">The spots seem to be on the feet, 2 spots, specifically between the toes and behind the Achilles.</mark>
