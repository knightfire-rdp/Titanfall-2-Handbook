# ℹ️ E-Smoke Mechanics

E-Smoke, aka. Electric Smoke is a powerful ability that is often overlooked, allowing you to deal extra damage to Titans, hide yourself from enemy attacks (especially as Ronin), and blind enemies by placing smoke in corridors and such. You can even use it to stop or kill a rodeoing enemy Pilot, instantly destroy traps, and even extinguish certain thermite attacks.&#x20;

Here's a full list of what it can do.

### <mark style="color:yellow;">E-Smoke Capabilities</mark>

* <mark style="color:yellow;">Deal preassigned damage to enemies.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Blind and disorient enemies.</mark>
  * <mark style="color:yellow;">Show Plasma Railgun glow.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Harm/kill a rodeoing enemy Pilot.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Instantly destroy Tether Traps and Tripwires.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Extinguish Firewall segments and Firestars.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Break Line of Sight lock on.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Spawn forwards or backwards.</mark>





## <mark style="color:yellow;">Deal Damage to Enemies</mark>

E-smoke damage is actually randomly preassigned to 3 different values. Which makes no sense since in the game files, e-smoke should deal only 450 Titan dps, being unable to give different damage values.

E-smoke damage alternates between 1100, 1400, and 1840 Titan damage (1845 with the Counter Ready kit), and it's preassigned randomly because the Counter Ready kit will have both of its smokes deal the same damage. So you could be unlucky and roll 1100 Titan damage smokes twice or be lucky and roll 1845 twice. The Counter Ready kit, for some reason, lets e-smoke deal a max of 5 more damage if you roll randomly high-damage smokes.

#### <mark style="color:yellow;">Key Features</mark>

* <mark style="color:yellow;">E-smoke Titan damage can roll one of 3 preassigned damage values:</mark>
  * <mark style="color:yellow;">Low 1100, medium 1400, high 1840 (1845 with Counter Ready kit).</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">The Counter Ready kit will have both of its smokes deal the same damage, depending on the roll.</mark>
  * <mark style="color:yellow;">The kit also will give you 5 more damage when rolled on high damage value.</mark>





## <mark style="color:yellow;">Blind and Disorient Enemies</mark>

E-smoke can help you flee from combat and also hide your Titan fully from enemy attacks or surprise the enemy with an attack, like a Power Shot, for example. Or when a Ronin jumps onto you and you need to reload, e-smoking and turning away helps to mitigate a ton of damage. You can also use it to mitigate damage from most cores by forcing the enemy to guess your position, giving you a chance for you to not be hit. Won't work on Salvo Core, though; if Tone places a crosshair on you once, the missiles will home in on you until the Tone looks away entirely.

You can use e-smoke to disorient an enemy or even multiple enemies at once. It happens a lot in team fights, where a Titan smokes in a narrow chokepoint, be it a corridor or an area full of action; the disorientation causes a lot of chaos, especially for Titans that need LoS to support their teammates, like Northstar or Legion.

E-Smoke is like a solid wall for AI, especially Auto-Titans; they won't even shoot in your direction, which is very useful to kill an Auto-Titan without getting punished by their unpredictable, high-damage melee lunges.

#### <mark style="color:yellow;">Key Features</mark>

* <mark style="color:yellow;">Flee from the enemy.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Cause enemies to miss their attacks and cores to some extend.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Conceal your next attack/move.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Disorient multiple enemies into chaos with well-placed smokes.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Block line of sight for enemy support Titans (Northstar, Legion).</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Literal wall for the Auto Titans; they won't even shoot through it.</mark>

### <mark style="color:yellow;">Show Plasma Railgun Glow</mark>

E-smoke does in fact show the "fully charged" Plasma Railgun. Though only at full charge, so a smart player can just fire at 4 charges or simply ads[^1] and unads to never reach the 5th charge level. This is still very useful to know in case you're not aware of it.

It doesn't work the same way for Legion's Power Shot; don't worry, you won't see a Power Shot glow through e-smoke.





## <mark style="color:yellow;">Harm/Kill a Rodeoing Enemy Pilot</mark>

Simple, the main intended use case of e-smoke is to smoke an enemy Pilot who tries to steal your battery. The Low Profile Pilot kit actually helps to delay the time for the Titan to know when there's a Pilot on board, letting the Pilot grab the battery without dying.&#x20;

Pilots can also bait the e-smoke to come out beforehand by faking a rodeo, basically rodeoing and instantly jumping off. But be aware that good players know how to turn 180 degrees quickly behind and melee kill the Pilot. So rodeoing against good players outside of combat is quite risky.

Occasionally it's better to not use e-smoke if you want to kill the pilot via a quick turn melee, giving you a battery + a Pilot kill. It happens a lot on Ronin with the Phase Dash ability.

#### <mark style="color:yellow;">Important to Note</mark>

* <mark style="color:yellow;">Pilots won't survive to pull the battery if the enemy reacts properly.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">The Low Profile Pilot kit lets Pilots pull a battery successfully if the enemy doesn't spot them beforehand.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">You can bait a Titan to deploy e-smoke by faking a rodeo.</mark>
* &#x20;<mark style="color:yellow;">Can save e-smoke and instead 180-degree melee the pilot with/without Phase Dash.</mark>





## <mark style="color:yellow;">Instantly destroy Tether Traps and Tripwires</mark>

Tether Traps are weird and can't be destroyed in the air by anything apart from e-smoke. E-smoke not only can vaporize airborne Tether Traps but also vaporizes Tether Traps on the floor and ones that are stuck to anything. Stuck in a Tether Trap? No more, shwooosh.

Ion's Tripwire is a bit odd; e-smoke destroys it nearly instantly, but it needs to hit the pylons themselves; the laser won't count. E-smoke will only destroy enemy Tripwires. It's not a good idea, though; it's something to know when placing e-smoke near Ion or when playing as Ion, as destroying the Tripwire causes it to explode, and its range is big enough to hit you when you destroy it with e-smoke.

#### <mark style="color:yellow;">Things to Keep in Mind</mark>

* <mark style="color:yellow;">Tether Traps of any kind get vaporized by e-smoke if they touch it before landing on the ground.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">E-smoke can instantly break attached and non-attached enemy Tether Traps.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">E-smoke instantly destroys enemy Tripwire pylons, causing explosive damage.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">You can purposely wait for the enemy to drop e-smoke and toss your Tripwire into it for free damage.</mark>





## <mark style="color:yellow;">Extinguish Firewall Segments and Firestars</mark>

So this is a very useful trick that you can use e-smoke for. You can use it to extinguish some thermite attacks, those being Firewall segments and Firestar. I said "Firewall segments" because it has a very strange property, since e-smoke will only extinguish segments of it. Firewall deals double the DPS of a canister, so it shouldn't be brushed off. Drop an e-smoke and create a safe passage through it if you don't want to lose some HP, or let the enemy know that you walked through it via hit markers.

Firestar on the other hand, is very necessary to use e-smoke on, as it deals nearly a bar of damage but can thankfully be extinguished instantly. **It's important to know that e-smoke extinguishes only on the first tick**, so when it first appears. If a Firewall or Firestar appears after the e-smoke is already placed, they won't be extinguished. E-smoke grenades can also extinguish these two.

#### <mark style="color:yellow;">What E-Smoke Can and</mark> <mark style="color:red;">Can't</mark> <mark style="color:yellow;">Extinguish</mark>

* <mark style="color:yellow;">Firewall Segements.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Attached Firestar.</mark>
* <mark style="color:red;">Not Firewall segments and Firestars that appear after e-smoke is already placed.</mark>
* <mark style="color:red;">Not T-203 Thermite droplets</mark> (will extinguish them only visually but will still deal damage)<mark style="color:red;">.</mark>
* <mark style="color:red;">Not Flame Trap.</mark>
* <mark style="color:red;">Not Flame Core.</mark>





## <mark style="color:yellow;">Break Line of Sight Lock-ons</mark>

E-smoke has the ability to blind your enemies but acts as a wall for Auto-Titans, so AI loses line of sight through E-smoke, even if a piece of the Titan pokes out. This is essential, since weapons are also blind to e-smoke, letting you break the lock-ons from Archer, Smart Core, and MTMS (Multi Target Missile System).

There is an enemy locking onto you, and you see a red lock warning? Just drop smoke. Though be aware, this won't do anything to Tone's Tracker missiles, Salvo Core (assuming she locked onto you beforehand), and, of course, already fired Archer/MTMS missiles. Dropping e-smoke might be a bad idea against Smart Core in some situations, because then the Legion won't end up shooting at chest-height obstacles instead of you (it aims for the body mass center), so keep that in mind.

#### <mark style="color:yellow;">Today I learned</mark>

* <mark style="color:yellow;">E-smoke can break lock-on from Archer, Smart Core, and MTMS.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Won't affect Tone's Rockets, Salvo Core, and already fired Archer and</mark> [<mark style="color:yellow;">MTMS</mark>](#user-content-fn-2)[^2]<mark style="color:yellow;">.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">E-Smoke is a useful escape-from-jail card for certain homing attacks.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Dropping e-smoke will let Legion free aim and not be blocked by chest-height obstacle.</mark>





## <mark style="color:yellow;">Spawn Forwards or Backwards</mark>

So e-smoke doesn't just spawn inside of you; it actually chooses whether to spawn, like, 70% behind you or in front of you. This does matter for breaking locks and killing Pilots and, of course, dealing more damage to Titans. E-smoke spawns out of one of those positions:

#### <mark style="color:yellow;">Front Position</mark> (eye position)

The Titan's cockpit uses a tracer to look for a possible space to place 240 units (like 6 meters) of e-smoke in front of your Titan. Imagine a **6-meter laser beam** shooting from your cockpit. If that laser doesn't hit any obstacle, e-smoke will spawn forward. The pitch at which you look, so looking up and down, doesn't affect the tracer. Only looking in a general horizontal direction influences the tracer view angle. The tracer also spawns from the same height as the cockpit, so uneven terrain won't be too much of an issue.

#### <mark style="color:yellow;">Back Position</mark> (origin)

The origin is essentially the opposite side of the eye position, meaning if the tracer from the cockpit hits any kind of obstacle within 6 meters, then the e-smoke will spawn from the origin position, so behind you. Most useful for rodeos or when fleeing.

#### <mark style="color:yellow;">Tracer Obstacle List</mark> (within 6 meters)

* <mark style="color:yellow;">Map Geometry</mark> (walls, chest-height walls, large slopes, buildings)<mark style="color:yellow;">.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Large map props</mark> (crates, vehicles, blue barrels?)<mark style="color:yellow;">.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Other Titans.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Large AI</mark> (Reapers, Dropships)<mark style="color:yellow;">.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Ceilings or overhangs.</mark>

#### <mark style="color:yellow;">None Tracer Obstacle List</mark>&#x20;

* <mark style="color:yellow;">Below Chest-Height Map Geometry.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Other Titans when using Phase Ability.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Pilots.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Small AI</mark> (Grunts, Spectres, and Stalkers)<mark style="color:yellow;">.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Drop Pods.</mark>



[^1]: ADS = "aim down sights" means aiming down with your gun.

[^2]: Multi-Target Missile System
