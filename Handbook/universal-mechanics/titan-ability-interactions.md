### <mark style="color:yellow;">**Legends**</mark>

<sup><mark style="color:orange;">**⟨1⟩**<mark style="color:orange;"></sup> The Thermite Canister and Tripwire values are for the already deployed Tripwires and ignited canisters. Midair Canisters will bounce off all defensive abilities and get destroyed fully by the Thermal Shield. Midair Tripwire will also bounce off all defensive abilities, but unlike the Canister, the Thermal Shield will cause it to explode, dealing damage to himself.\
\
<sup><mark style="color:orange;">**⟨2⟩**<mark style="color:orange;"></sup> Vortex Shield and Thermal Shield will block Arc Wave and Firewall, but due to Scorch's smaller shield radius, he needs to look slightly downwards to block it better. \
Flame Core can also be blocked, although it's fired in 3 spread-out Arc-Wave-like waves, and you must be careful to not let a single wave hit your side; otherwise, you'll take full damage. Blocking Flame Core is easier in tight corridors or with fewer segments.<br>

<sup><mark style="color:orange;">**⟨3⟩**<mark style="color:orange;"></sup> The Gun Shield will actually survive an Arc Wave if you move it out of the way fast enough; you'll sometimes see the Gun Shield take big damage if you get it out of the way mid-hit.<br>

<sup><mark style="color:orange;">**⟨4⟩**<mark style="color:orange;"></sup> The initial missile will directly impact nearly every defensive ability. If it's a Vortex Shield, it'll get caught; the Thermal Shield will destroy it fully, but the Gun Shield and Particle Wall will act like a surface against which Cluster Missile will explode and create sub-explosions that ignore shields.&#x20;
