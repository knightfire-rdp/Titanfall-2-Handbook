# ℹ️ Rodeo Guard

## <mark style="color:yellow;">What is Rodeo Guard?</mark>

An interesting mechanic that lets a Pilot rodeo a teammate Titan with an overshield for immunity to nearly all damage. Surprisingly this even works when rodeoing a teammate Auto-Titan, but even better; you don't need an overshield.

The rodeoing Pilot can even survive cluster missile explosions despite being in reach of them. Do keep in mind that a rodeoing Pilot becomes an extension of the overshield's hitbox, meaning you can shoot the pilot to damage the overshield.

Rodeo Guard might not sound useful, but do keep in mind that overshield is immune to crit damage and works with Sword Block and Sword Core Sword Block for damage reduction. The immunity to almost all types of damage allows for more strategies using pilots on rodeo, especially when combined with MGL, Thunderbolt, and Archer.&#x20;

Put the Pilot on a shielded Ronin that is fighting another Ronin, and your Ronin will get a massive advantage. From the moment the enemy Ronin drops block, he'll get hit by MGL grenades or get punished by the Ronin. Hence why it's a good pressure weapon.

Obviously Rodeo Guard doesn't work when rodeoing an enemy Titan/Auto-Titan, but there are few actual factors that can prevent Rodeo Guard from working. There are 2 cases that prevent Rodeo Guard. It's during the rodeo start animation, when you try to get on the Titan there will be a short animation. Another case is when the player controlling the Titan dies, so you won't get any immunity from the Auto-Titan, even if it has an overshield.

### <mark style="color:yellow;">Key Features</mark>

* <mark style="color:yellow;">Rodeo Guard makes Pilot immune to nearly all sources of damage.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Rodeo Guard works by having a Pilot rodeo you while having an overshield or simply by rodeoing an Auto-Titan regardless of having an overshield or not.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">The rodeoing Pilot becomes part of the overshield hitbox, extending its size.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Works with Sword Block damage reduction and allows for some interesting strategies.</mark>
* <mark style="color:red;">Doesn't work when rodeoing an enemy Titan/Auto-Titan.</mark>
* <mark style="color:red;">Pilot is not immune during the short animation when getting on the Titan/Auto-Titan.</mark>
* <mark style="color:red;">Pilot is not immune when rodeoing the Auto-Titan of a dead player.</mark>
