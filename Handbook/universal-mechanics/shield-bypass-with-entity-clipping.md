# 🟢 Shield Bypass with Entity Clipping

> Special thanks to starspace225 for pointing out in the Lighthouse Discord server that you can bypass shields using a broken Frontier Defense sentry. Then Tilaly brought to me the news that you can phase into Legion as Ronin and let Legion bypass shields with his gun.&#x20;

## <mark style="color:yellow;">What is Shield Bypass with Entity Clipping?</mark>

This tech lets you quite literally shoot straight through Vortex Shield, Thermal Shield, Particle Wall, Gun Shield, and Hard Cover.

It seems to occur when you aim at an enemy while another entity is clipping into your head (not exactly inside your head, but where your bullets originate from). The game registers both the entity in your head and the enemy you're targeting as being hit by your shots.

This happens because you're in an awkward position where the game can't tell the difference between the two. This explains why you don’t see tracers appear towards the enemy player or the usual impact effects (since they show up in your face instead).

The glitch lets you hit enemies behind all solid shields except for Amped Wall and Dome Shield. Shields are solid objects, not entities, so the game ends up targeting the enemy behind the shield instead. This might be why the glitch doesn’t bypass Amped Wall and Dome Shield, which are an entity, not just a solid object. This is just my theory though.&#x20;

{% hint style="info" %}
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Sword Block is NOT a shield, so it doesn't work, nor will it be posted here.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">World terrain obstacles do NOT count as entities.</mark>
{% endhint %}



### <mark style="color:yellow;">Pilot Methods to Perform the Glitch</mark>

Do keep in mind that you need to be partially inside of the entity, so you're very much killable, especially to a Vortex Shield reflect.... \
The glitch applies to all hitscan[^1] guns, including the Charge Rifle.

**List of Things That a Pilot Needs to Crouch Inside Of to Make This Glitch Work:**

1. <mark style="color:yellow;">Any Amped Wall, crouching being optional.</mark> (More consistent when it's backwards at an angle.)
2. <mark style="color:yellow;">Own/teammate Titan's feet, standing is harder.</mark>
3. <mark style="color:yellow;">At least 2 batteries, like 4-5 without crouching.</mark><sup><mark style="color:orange;">**⟨1⟩**<mark style="color:orange;"></sup>
4. <mark style="color:yellow;">Teammate Pilot; inconsistent since you must move constantly as you get pushed back.</mark>
5. <mark style="color:yellow;">Friendly Grunt/Spectre.</mark> (Incredibly inconsistent, they also move away from you, so good luck.)
6. <mark style="color:yellow;">Any standing Holo Pilot hologram.</mark>
7. <mark style="color:yellow;">Own/friendly Tripwire pylon, crouching being optional.</mark>
8. <mark style="color:yellow;">Any unlit Thermite Canister.</mark>
9. <mark style="color:yellow;">Own/friendly anti-Pilot/Titan sentry.</mark><sup><mark style="color:orange;">**⟨2⟩**<mark style="color:orange;"></sup>
10. <mark style="color:yellow;">Having the head inside of a friendly satchel placed on a walk-through lamppost, crouching is optional.</mark><sup><mark style="color:orange;">**⟨3⟩**<mark style="color:orange;"></sup>
11. <mark style="color:yellow;">Any Dome Shield; it's easier while standing.</mark>

**Extra Info:**

<mark style="color:yellow;background-color:$success;">A. Can amp your shots and also bypass multiple shields.</mark>

<mark style="color:yellow;background-color:$success;">B. Charge Rifle working, no visible beam.</mark><sup><mark style="color:orange;">**⟨4⟩**<mark style="color:orange;"></sup>

<mark style="color:$warning;background-color:$danger;">C. Can't do the glitch: Vortex Shield, Thermal Shield, Particle Wall, Gun Shield, Hard Cover.</mark>

<mark style="color:$warning;background-color:$danger;">D. Can't bypass: Amped Wall, Dome Shield.</mark>

{% hint style="info" %}
<sup><mark style="color:orange;">**⟨1⟩**<mark style="color:orange;"></sup> <mark style="color:yellow;">This is the only method where you're unable to amp your shots, if you do, your shots will not bypass the shield but instead only create an impact effect.</mark>

<sup><mark style="color:orange;">**⟨2⟩**<mark style="color:orange;"></sup> <mark style="color:yellow;">Pilot sentry can do the glitch if the Pilot or an Amped Wall is inside of it; the turret also becomes fully automatic through the Amped Wall as a nice bonus. But it won't attack a Titan unless shot by one.</mark>

<sup><mark style="color:orange;">**⟨3⟩**<mark style="color:orange;"></sup> <mark style="color:yellow;">Shooting own satchels causes them to explode, and by this method a white hitmarker impact effect appears when hitting through a Particle Wall, Gun Shield, and Hard Cover. But a red one appears on the Vortex Shield and Thermal Shield.</mark>

<sup><mark style="color:orange;">**⟨4⟩**<mark style="color:orange;"></sup> <mark style="color:yellow;">I know that I said that hitscan weapons work, but Charge Rifle working is so weird and interesting to me, it also looks very silly.</mark>
{% endhint %}

### <mark style="color:yellow;">Titan Methods to Perform the Glitch</mark>

The glitch applies only to Predator Cannon and XO-16; no other abilities or weapons work, not even close range Power Shot.

**Here’s the List of Things That a Titan Needs to Be Inside Of to Make This Glitch Work:**

1. <mark style="color:yellow;">Teammate Titan while crouching.</mark><sup><mark style="color:orange;">**⟨1⟩**<mark style="color:orange;"></sup>
2. <mark style="color:yellow;">Amped Wall clipping through Titan's head;</mark> <mark style="color:yellow;">it's easier while crouching.</mark>
3. <mark style="color:yellow;">At least 7 batteries while crouching; need 10 to do it while standing.</mark>
4. <mark style="color:yellow;">Having the Titan's head inside of a friendly satchel placed on a walk-through lamppost.</mark><sup><mark style="color:orange;">**⟨2⟩**<mark style="color:orange;"></sup>
5. <mark style="color:yellow;">Any Dome Shield; it's easier while standing.</mark><sup><mark style="color:orange;">**⟨3⟩**<mark style="color:orange;"></sup>

**Extra Info:**

<mark style="color:yellow;background-color:$success;">A. Can bypass multiple shields.</mark><sup><mark style="color:orange;">**⟨4⟩**<mark style="color:orange;"></sup>

<mark style="color:$warning;background-color:$danger;">B. Can't do the glitch: Vortex Shield, Thermal Shield, Particle Wall, Gun Shield, Hard Cover.</mark>

<mark style="color:$warning;background-color:$danger;">C. Can't bypass: Amped Wall, Dome Shield.</mark>

<mark style="color:$warning;background-color:$danger;">D. Broken close range Power Shot.</mark><sup><mark style="color:orange;">**⟨5⟩**<mark style="color:orange;"></sup>

{% hint style="info" %}
<sup><mark style="color:orange;">**⟨1⟩**<mark style="color:orange;"></sup> <mark style="color:yellow;">This method is a lot easier with Ronin since he can phase inside of you, but still doable if another Titan gets inside you by spamming crouch and walking into you.</mark>

<sup><mark style="color:orange;">**⟨3⟩**<mark style="color:orange;"></sup> <mark style="color:yellow;">Standing makes it way easier, but if you want to crouch, first find it from standing position, then crouch and walk a tiny bit forward, literally the opposite way compared to Holo Pilot Hologram.</mark>
