# 🚧 Overshield Fully in Depth

Overshield is a rather complex piece of a secondary health pool. Not only does it offer an additional bar of health, it also has a ton of additional features.

### <mark style="color:yellow;">Overshield Capabilities</mark>

* Additional bar of health.
* Ease of obtaining.
* Ease of losing it.
* Immunity to crits, but prone to Pilot weapons.
* Altered core gain.
* Protection for the rodeoing Pilot.
* Immunity to rodeo death + rodeo damage reduction.
