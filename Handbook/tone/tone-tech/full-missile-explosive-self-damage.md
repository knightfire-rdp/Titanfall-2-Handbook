# 🔻 Full Missile Explosive Self-Damage

This is so bad that I may consider it a disadvantageous tech. The Tone's Tracker Rockets have 100% self-damage, meaning if you hit something right in your face with your rockets, you'll deal the same damage as if they were to hit someone with missile explosive damage. Which is thankfully not as much as direct impact damage. It's actually a 40% damage difference, but it's still a lot to consider.

This is an actual strategy that some Titans will do, especially Monarch or Legion (Gun Shield mainly). Having you splash rockets against them to help in dooming you. It's probably used by Tones when fighting other Tones too. It doesn't do much against Ion and Scorch though; their shields catch or destroy the rockets.

**This surprisingly is not the case for Salvo Core; it won't deal self-damage to you.**
