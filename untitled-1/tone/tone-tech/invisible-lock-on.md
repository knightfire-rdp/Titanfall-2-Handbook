# 🟢 Invisible Lock-On

When a Tone reapplies the lock-on before it would expire naturally, the lock-on message will disappear for the enemy, but they will still be locked on for you. This works with a 40 mm shot and even sonar ability.

This tech is incredibly strong, especially against opponents that aren't pushing you and instead are poking at you from a distance. Letting you catch them off guard with missiles coming out of nowhere. The enemy normally relies on the lock-on warning to go away to know when the Tracker Rockets got fired.

### <mark style="color:yellow;">Key Features</mark>

* <mark style="color:yellow;">A lock-on lasts 15 seconds.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Applying a lock before the 15-second mark will let you refresh the timer.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">The lock-on warning message will disappear after 15 sec and cannot be refreshed.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Can use either the 40 mm Tracker Cannon or Sonar Lock to refresh the lock.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">You can theoretically keep refreshing the lock-on forever.</mark>
