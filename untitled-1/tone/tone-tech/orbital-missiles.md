# 🟢 Orbital Missiles

Orbital missiles quite literally match the term “orbital.” The tech causes your Tracking Rockets to fly blindly in one direction for several seconds before regaining sight and coming back for the target.

This tech can be easily done by simply being in melee or straight in the face range, being backward, or at a sharp angle away from the enemy before firing. Depending on where you look, the missiles will fly in that direction for a sec; this has many applications.

The main application of this tech is to have the missiles fly into orbit and return later when the enemy won't be expecting them. The other application is using them as a better Monarch Rocket Salvo. Simply aim at a nearby target and hit them with the missiles; you can effortlessly catch the enemy off guard.&#x20;

It's important to note that once rockets are fired, the lock-on warning immediately gets removed; hence, they can catch the opponent off guard if they come back after a few seconds.

### <mark style="color:yellow;">Step-by-Step Sequence</mark>

1. <mark style="color:yellow;">Be in melee or right in the face range.</mark>
2. <mark style="color:yellow;">Look away from the target so that you're backwards to them or at a sharp angle away.</mark>
3. <mark style="color:yellow;">Fire the missiles either up into the sky or at a different target.</mark>
