# 🔰 P2W Prime Execution

:beginner:”You can reset your ammo up to 10 shots, but you can also reset your ammo down from 12 to 10 shots.”

You know how Tone has 12 shots in a magazine? What if you're low on ammo and want to reload your gun to 10 out of 12 shots during an execution? Well, now you can, featuring the Tone Prime execution. Simply execute any opponent, and your magazine will be set to 10 shots after the execution. Not a full magazine, but still incredibly powerful. And yeah, kind of a pay-to-lose too, if you execute someone with a full magazine and lose 2 shots.

This only applies to Tone Prime execution and is not affected by the enemy leaving mid-match. Strangely enough, the execution shows the Tone firing 3 shots, yet your magazine loses only 2 shots, a weird anomaly. This also works with the Frontier Defense Aegis upgrade kit, letting you refill 20 out of 22 shots.

#### <mark style="color:yellow;">Key Features</mark>

* <mark style="color:yellow;">Applicable only to Prime Tone execution.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Refreshes the magazine to 10 out of 12 shots every time you execute.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Having a full magazine will still cause a refresh.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Not accurate to the 3-shot execution animation.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Applies to the Frontier Defense Aegis Tone upgrade kit too.</mark>
