# 🔰 Sonar Melee Cancel

:beginner:”Ability to force your sonar pod up and to melee at the cost of despawning the Sonar Lock and making the cooldown invisible.”

A weird tech that lets you pop up your sonar pod and melee instead of shooting a Sonar Lock in case you deployed it accidentally and need to melee instead. But at the same time you'll despawn the Sonar Lock and bug out the cooldown ability icon. It won't show you the cooldown progress nor will it make a sound when you try to activate early when it's not available. So you'll need to blindly guess when it's back.

For that reason, you should always use this tech with caution. It does, however, have some niche use cases for fighting Ronin or Ion, deploying the pod might catch them off guard thinking you're firing Tracker Rockets.

### <mark style="color:yellow;">Key Features</mark>

* <mark style="color:yellow;">Melee canceling Sonar Lock despawns it and lets you melee instead of deploying it.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Also lets you pop up the sonar pod which enemies can mistake for a rocket pod.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">The despawned Sonar Lock will also break your cooldown icon, not showing the cooldown.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Very niche tech that can let you disorient enemies, specifically Ronin and Ion in close range.</mark>
