# 🟢 Missile Chaining

Missile chaining is a term for continuously firing infinite missiles at locked-on targets via disembarking mid-fire animation. If done right, it's infinite. It's a borderline exploit.&#x20;

> I was quite hesitant about documenting this tech because I considered it too powerful, but since it's allowed in the competitive LTS scene, I may as well document it.

You just need some distance, the ability to disembark, and a locked-on target or more. Simply disembarking mid-fire animation bugs Tone out to think the missiles never fired in the first place. You can use this tech to literally fire volleys of endless missiles into the sky.&#x20;

### <mark style="color:yellow;">To Note</mark>

* <mark style="color:yellow;">You require at least one locked-on target.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">You need to be some distance away; the farther, the more consistent.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Furthermore, you need to disembark mid-fire animation and immediately embark, NOT after the fire animation.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Difficult to do it consistently.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Phase Embark Pilot kit is required.</mark>
