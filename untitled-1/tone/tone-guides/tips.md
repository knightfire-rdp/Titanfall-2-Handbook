{% tab title="Berna 1" %}
### <mark style="color:yellow;">Bernadotte</mark>

* Patience is the most important aspect of Tone. You decide when to fire Tracking Rockets and at what angle, use this to hit Titans you normally wouldn’t be able to.
* The two most important things with Tone is 40mm aim and Tracker Rocket curves. Good 40mm aim means more locks which means more damage and pressure onto the enemy team.
* Vertical Map Knowledge: you need to know what buildings or objects on buildings will block your curves and curve accordingly to get around them.
* Try not to use the same curve twice (more relevant in competitive LTS, in public lobbies you can get away with repeating curves).
* Try to hit Titans with "flicky" defensives like Ion and Ronin (Scorch as well but less important) with Tracking Rockets when they’re doing something else like fighting a teammate.
* Tone is scariest when she has locks or is about to get locks.
* Tracking Rockets are a slower offensive ability unlike many of the other offensives in Titanfall. Thus Tone has to play literal RPS when firing them. Tone is all about what she is going to do vs. what her opponent is going to do. With that you can condition people to commit to certain options once they think they know what you’re going to do and use their judgment against them at crucial moments.
* Combine your damage with your teammates. I.E. the enemy Ronin is fighting your Ronin; fire Tracking Rockets as your Ronin melees which forces the enemy Ronin to make a choice: block the Tracking Rockets and eat the melee or pin the melee but take damage from Tracking Rockets.
* Tracking Rockets deal 2,800 damage with Rocket Barrage, which is more than a bar of health or a doombar (2,500). Use this to pressure doomed Titans even more or as a damage dump option to outright kill a full doom bar Titan.
* Tone is a Titan with uptime and downtime. Know your timing accordingly. Seek cover and reposition before Particle Wall expires as well as fire Tracking Rockets before they expire.
* If you’re trying to kill an enemy Titan outright, try to doom them when they’re low with 40mm before using Tracking Rockets (if you’re on Rocket Barrage) as you’ll deal more damage than their doom bar (2,800 damage vs. 2,500 health). Alternatively, you can do a spread out curve/split the Tracking Rockets apart to bypass doom invulnerability and deal damage to their doom bar.&#x20;
* The 40mm is an excellent pilot killer. The projectile is huge for hitting direct shots, and the splash damage can even force pilots out of cover if they sit for too long.
* Use Sonar Pulse to flush out enemy Pilots in buildings so your teammates can kill them. It also lets you track Cloak Pilots effectively.
* Tone can mitigate damage without Particle Wall. Use natural cover, teammates, and enemies to block damage when Particle Wall is on cooldown. I.E., position so one enemy is in front of the other, so the one in the back’s damage is blocked.

{% tab title="Berna 2" %}
* Take power positions, only Ronin can force you out. Places like hill on Colony.
* Tone loves cross-shots, being on the side lets you get more locks and helps teammate fights. Know when to push up though, sitting on the side forever loses rounds.
* Coordinate with your teammates when you have locks to force enemies into catch-22s (no good decisions) This forces the enemy to pay attention to locks and your teammate, which can lead to mistakes.
* Coordinate with your teammates to force cooldowns before you use locks. I.E. your teammate forces Northstar to hover and then you use your Tracking Rockets once it’s over.
* Call when you have locks on Ion and Ronin, this lets your team capitalize on their distraction. Important because of how their defensives work.
* Call when Ion catches (i.e., you threw them and no hit markers). Better to assume they got caught rather than missed.
* Bully Ronin at range, locks make it easy to force phase, so your Ronin has an advantage.
* Tone’s job is to take a lane and depending on what’s there, hold and play slow (Northstar or Ronin) or kill what’s there with Salvo Core and make the game a 3v2 or 4v3 (Tone or Legion). If Ion is holding it, you can either play slow and whittle her down or push and take the 1v1.
* If Ronin pushes you, your job is to die slowly. You’re not going to win unless they throw so make the fight last as long as possible, wasting the Ronin’s time and whittling his health down. If the Ronin must use Sword Core to kill you, you won.
* Use Particle Wall to anchor teammate pulls if you need to, better than nothing.
* Use Sonar Pulse at the start of the game for free information.
* Use Sonar Pulse to help with teammate dash shots. Giving Northstar or Legion free wallhacks for Railgun and Power Shot is broken.

## <mark style="color:yellow;">Tone vs. Ion Tips</mark>

{% tabs %}
{% tab title="Info 3" %}
This is simply a cover to make navigation easier. You can click through the different tabs to view tips done by different people.

<mark style="color:yellow;">Berna:</mark> <mark style="color:yellow;">Tone vs. Ion Tips from</mark> <mark style="color:yellow;">Bernadotte.</mark>
{% endtab %}

{% tab title="Berna 3" %}
### <mark style="color:yellow;">Bernadotte</mark>

* Tone decides the pace of the fight, whether that’s slow/poke or fast/aggressive.
* Tone has to match Ion’s style of play. If Ion is using Vortex more than Splitter Rifle then you can rely on 40mm and chip. If she’s very aggressive with Laser Shot and Splitter Rifle then you need to make reads with Tracking Rockets, predicting when the Ion isn’t going to use Vortex.
* When you see Ion at range, use Particle Wall preemptively as she’s likely to Laser Shot.
* Ion has no answer for Particle Wall aside from charging at you or disengaging, both are wins.
* When she’s walking towards you holding Vortex, you can splash her back left foot to get locks but you must shoot where she’s going to walk.
* Vortex has a delay after it’s reflected something, you can shoot one 40mm into Vortex and then wait for Ion to reflect it, and this will get you one free 40mm shot.
* Make use of melee cancelling Tracking Rockets , the pod sticks up and she’ll have to read when you’re going to use them instead of reacting. This guarantees you at least partial hits if not a full salvo with proper timing.
* Good players are conditioned to look up for Tracking Rockets, look down and shoot them under the shield to mix them up.
* Use curves that come out fast. Slower curves give Ion time to catch them. Shooting them straight ahead and looking straight up then shooting are your best bets.
* At mid to close range, use Particle Wall, get locks, and melee her out of wall as she tries to get past, mixing in 40mm and Tracking Rockets  as necessary.
* Conditioning and reads are very important. Condition the Ion to do what you want (hold Vortex, melee, shoot, etc.) and switch up on her when she gets too comfortable/thinks she’s figured you out. For reads, figure out what the Ion’s pattern is and punish them for sticking to it.
* You can feed one 40mm into Vortex and read when she’s going to drop it, preemptively using Tracking Rockets to guarantee they all land. You can also just read when she’s going to drop Vortex but that’s more risky.\
  Save your dash to avoid Tripwires or to dodge a Tracker Rocket reflect.
* When Salvo coring an Ion, make use of Salvo Core coming in three parts. Throw the first part into her Vortex to drain her energy, then look up and drop parts two and three on her.
* Vortex can despawn Salvo Core, if the Ion is trying to do so (hugging you tight and holding Vortex), melee her until you have a dash, then 180 and dash away while coring into the air.
* Salvo Core will beat Laser Core every time. You get access to your other abilities sooner than Ion so you can melee her if she’s doomed or shoot Tracking Rockets.
* If the Ion prefers to poke you out with Laser Shot, you have two options. Either play passive with her, using Sonar Pulse and Particle Wall to beat Laser Shot or take on Laser Shot purposefully then push her when she has half energy. You should win because of her energy deficit.
* When Ion is occupied with one of your teammates, try to curve Tracking Rockets into her back by looking far and slightly up to the left or right. This forces her to either turn around to catch the rockets or continue vortexing your teammate.

## <mark style="color:yellow;">Tone vs. Northstar Tips</mark>

{% tabs %}
{% tab title="Info 5" %}

<mark style="color:yellow;">Camp: Tone vs. Northstar Tips from Campareous.</mark>
{% endtab %}

{% tab title="Berna 5" %}
### <mark style="color:yellow;">Bernadotte</mark>

* Mainly a positioning game.
* Practice reacting to dash shots (Northstar dashes out, shoots, and then dashes back into cover) with Particle Wall (if you have it) or dashes (if you don’t) to avoid the shot.
* Use Sonar Pulse to find where she’s hiding and play accordingly outside of her line-of-sight. This will help so she has to come out to hit you or break Particle Wall.
* Hover and Flight Core mess up Tracker Rocket tracking. Tracking Rockets auto-go to the enemy’s chest. Northstar’s smaller chest combined with their movement can lead to Tracking Rockets missing. Try to use Tracking Rockets  after Hover ends or curve them in the direction she’s going, I.E. if she’s flying left, look left and curve them to the right to hit her. The same goes for if you’re getting cored. You can also dash underneath her and fire them straight up to hit her.
* Use rocket curves that force her to dash out of cover or towards you so you can continue shooting her, like back turn or spread out curves (refer to Tracker Rocket section by Lethal).
* If you can get into a good position, just sit there and force her to come to you. When she goes for a shot, use Particle Wall to block and then push her for damage, similar to Ion and Laser Shot.&#x20;
* Alternative to the previous tip, you can just push her and eat the shot, then use Particle Wall to take the space.
* When you’re in her face, walk/sprint in one direction then dash to the other to bait the Railgun shot and dodge it.
* Because of how Hover messes with Tracker Rocket tracking, you can get easy orbital rockets on her. Try to curve the rockets past her during Hover if you want to do it (clip here).
* You’re unlikely to land full damage on her with Tracking Rockets so prioritize spread out curves for splash damage (refer to Tracker Rocket section by Lethal).

* Locate the Northstar, remember the most common peak spots, and sonar them.
* Bait dashes so they can't dodge, like when they're close to you to get distance, you can profit on them exhausting their dashes, like catching them off position.
* When salvo coring, aim from directly above from the sky or right below her. But for most consistency, chase the Northstar's hover direction with the missiles. If she flies towards the left, curve salvo core to fly towards her from the right side.&#x20;

## <mark style="color:yellow;">Tone vs. Ronin Tips</mark>

{% tabs %}
{% tab title="Info 6" %}

{% tab title="Berna 6" %}
### <mark style="color:yellow;">Bernadotte</mark>

<mark style="color:yellow;">**General Stuff**</mark>

* Four key elements: on point 40mm aim, damage mitigation, on point melee boxes, and tracking Arc Wave and Phase Dash cooldowns (11 seconds and 12/8 for Phase Dash (8 with Temporal Anomaly)).
* Two ways to play this matchup: you can just fight him and try to read when he’s going to shoot or use Arc Wave and using Tracking Rockets preemptively (using Crouch Tech for faster deployment time). The second way is more methodical, I.E. once you get locks use Particle Wall and then use Tracking Rockets when he uses Arc Wave.
* Good 40mm aims means more locks which means more pressure on the Ronin so he can’t just aggro you.
* Utilize Stutter ADSing to mitigate damage from Leadwall.
* With Crouch Tech Ronin can shoot you when you melee and block all of a Tracking Rocket salvo, be careful of meleeing when he has ammo.
* Don’t waste 40mm ammo once you have locks, you’ll need every bullet. The exception is when Ronin is doomed and you’ll only get one salvo out.
* As Ronin approaches, try to get locks and play around cooldowns. He’ll catch you eventually but you can play distance early on and chip him down before he engages.
* Know when it’s your time to attack. When Ronin doesn’t have Phase Dash or no Leadwall you can be aggressive with melees and Tracking Rockets.

<mark style="color:yellow;">**Tracking Rockets**</mark>

* You need to land one salvo of Tracking Rockets to win, easier said than done against top Ronins.
* Blocked and partial Tracking Rocket hits are just as valuable, especially if a full salvo is blocked.
* When Phase Dash is coming back up you can using Tracking Rockets and get partial connects before Ronin phases.
* When Ronin phases, you can still land Tracking Rockets. Throw them at a wide and spread out curve to delay them so when Ronin comes out of Phase they still hit him.
* When Ronin dashes at a distance, do the same wide spread out curve to hit him when his dash finishes, can catch impatient Ronins off guard.
* You can dash and fire Tracking Rockets when Ronin dashes and shoots to maximize rockets connected, along with mitigating damage from Leadwall. Be wary of Arc Waves though.
* When Ronin is close, do straightforward curves like straight ahead, you need to land damage and spread out curves delay your damage, letting Ronin hit you before your damage lands.

<mark style="color:yellow;">**Particle Wall**</mark>

* You can bait Arc Waves by placing Particle Wall and then dashing.
* You can walk in one direction and then dash in the opposite direction to bait Arc Waves.
* You can time Particle to where it won’t be broken by Arc Wave. Place it as the Arc Wave hits and travels through you. This can catch Ronins who use Arc Wave and then Leadwall immediately off guard.
* Use your dash or dashes to dodge Arc Wave, using them otherwise can be risky.
* Particle Wall is not going to stay up, accept that and use it to block one or two Leadwall shots before dodging the Arc Wave.
* Be aware of how many shots Ronin has, you don’t want to use Particle Wall when he has none.

<mark style="color:yellow;">**Salvo Core**</mark>

* You need to get Salvo Core when he gets Sword Core to pressure it out; even being 10% off can mean you lose the fight.
* If Ronin doesn’t respect Salvo Core and is just trying to kill you with Sword Core, aim Salvo Core at his toes and track him, you’ll deal more damage faster.
* If Ronin does respect Salvo Core then you need to balance melees with bringing Salvo Core. I.E. pin a melee and then bring core towards him before bringing it back up to bait him. Clip here.
* When you have Salvo Core and Ronin is coring you, you can throw the first half into him to force him to back off and then bring the rest of it into the air to pressure Ronin.
{% endtab %}

{% tab title="Camp 6" %}
### <mark style="color:yellow;">Campareous</mark>

* If Ronin runs Thunderstorm, then your walls won't do much unless you get an anti-Arc Wave spot.
* It's fine to go for anti-Arc Wave spots temporarily; otherwise, you'll get 4 leadwall critted.
* Keep a dash on hand to dash away&#x20;
* Ronin is stronger in 1v1s against Tone because he can play patiently around the locks, which helps Ronin beat Tone. But in a team fight, Tone has an advantage.

## <mark style="color:yellow;">Tone vs. Tone Tips</mark>

{% tab title="Berna 7" %}
### <mark style="color:yellow;">Bernadotte</mark>

* Another positioning game but resource management is important (ammo, cooldowns, core, and health).
* Know when to push when you have advantage (more ammo, more core, more health, more cooldowns (mainly Particle Wall). When done properly you’ll win every time.
* Be aware of where the enemy Tone can shoot to splash damage you to get locks and position accordingly. This goes the other way as well, be aware of where you can shoot to splash damage the enemy Tone.
* When the enemy Tone is trying to shoot the left or right of your Particle Wall to get locks, walk the opposite direction instead of trying to AD strafe (walk left when they shoot right, walk right when they shoot left).
* When you’re fighting up close don’t be afraid to use Particle Wall to block Tracking Rockets. Even though it’ll get destroyed it saves you a bar of health. You’re unlikely to get enough distance that the enemy won’t be able to walk through it so this is a better use.
* Use Stutter ADSing when fighting up close to make the enemy Tone whiff, if you’re ahead by one Tracking Rocket Salvo you should win.
* If you have locks and no Particle Wall, hold your locks. When the enemy Tone places Particle Wall break it with Tracking Rockets so the fight is even. If you fire the Tracking Rockets  before the enemy Tone uses Particle Wall you lose because they’ll place it and you’ll have no way to break it.
* If the enemy Tone just tries to barrel towards you once you place Particle Wall, just get locks and do back turn curves to get damage when they dash as they try to get close.
* Reinforced Particle Wall doesn’t break to one Tracker Rocket salvo. If you notice the enemy Tone is using it, prioritize curving Tracking Rockets over the wall instead of breaking it.
* If the enemy Tone tries to hug you so you damage yourself with Tracking Rockets , either do orbital rockets or melee then walk backwards while firing Tracking Rockets .
* You can hug the enemy Tone to get them to damage themselves with their own Tracking Rockets. If you fire locks, get them to splash themselves, and then fire locks again you’ll doom them.
* Don’t fire Tracking Rockets  straight at the enemy Tone, even without dashes they can still dodge by sprinting forward at a 45 degree angle
* When fighting up close, try to predict what direction they’re going to dash before or as you fire Tracking Rockets.
* Try to bait Tracking Rockets by walking in one direction and then dashing in the other to mitigate damage.
* Save your dashes to dodge Tracking Rockets, using them for movement in secondary in this matchup.
* Salvo Core does reduced damage when hitting Particle Wall; prioritize throwing it into the air and then dropping it on the enemy Tone.
* See tech section for despawning Salvo Core.

## <mark style="color:yellow;">Tone vs. Legion</mark> <mark style="color:yellow;">Tips</mark>

{% tabs %}
{% tab title="Info 8" %}

<mark style="color:yellow;">Berna:</mark> <mark style="color:yellow;">Tone vs. Legion Tips from</mark> <mark style="color:yellow;">Bernadotte.</mark>
{% endtab %}

{% tab title="Berna 8" %}
### <mark style="color:yellow;">Bernadotte</mark>

* All about positioning, you want to play at mid to maintain pressure on Legion but not too close so he can push you and not too far that you lose track of him.
* Don’t use Particle Wall at the first source of damage, save it for the biggest source of damage: Power Shot.
* Land your damage as Legion approaches, if Legion uses Gun Shield break it and just kill him.
* Using Overcore, you get Salvo Core in four salvos or three with a battery, combined with how easy it is to locks on Legion he has a very hard time.
* Gun Shield doesn’t cover all of Legion, you can splash his feet for locks.
* Tracking Rockets deal 2800 damage with Rocket Barrage and Gun Shield has 2500 health (no Bulwark) so you can break it every time. If Legion uses Gun Shield in the middle of the salvo to preserve it, splash his feet for locks.
* Gun Shield can despawn Salvo Core so be careful of coring in Legion’s face.
* If Legion is trying to despawn Salvo Core, you can walk backwards while coring his toes to avoid the despawn.
* If you need to break Gun Shield before Legion gets close and you don’t have locks then you can use Salvo Core to break it.
* Salvo Core lasts longer than Gun Shield, abuse this fact. Aim Salvo Core into the sky and then look at the Legion, when they use Gun Shield and look into the sky shoot them with the 40mm once and then bring the core back into the sky. Repeat this two more times and Gun Shield will have expired and you’ll still have Salvo Core and locks to kill Legion with. You’ll have to keep Salvo Core close though; don’t let it fly too far out into the sky.
