<sup><mark style="color:orange;">**⟨1⟩**<mark style="color:orange;"></sup>The Dome Shield can fit inside several friendly Titans and Pilots, due to its nature of being invulnerable to damage, with the only exception being Titan melee being able to go through the shield. The dome-shielded Titan will still be immune to any damage, even if another Titan falls on it.\
\
The Dome Shield will disappear early if you embark (standing still and looking around won't disable the shield) and move your Titan or when you wake up your Titan by using the guard/follow mode key.
