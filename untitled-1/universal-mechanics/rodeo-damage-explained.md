# ℹ️ Rodeo Damage Explained

Rodeo damage can be useful to instantly kill a doomed Titan or just grab a battery. Rodeo damage also ignores Ronin's Sword Block, so it's a useful thing, assuming they [don't have phase ready.](../ronin/ronin-tech/anti-rodeo-phase-tech.md) Damage to your Titan from rodeos is dependent on 4 different things.

### <mark style="color:yellow;">Things That Affect Rodeo Damage</mark>

* <mark style="color:yellow;">Titan state and rodeo type.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Whether overshield is present.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">The amount of HP in doomstate with overshield.</mark>



## <mark style="color:yellow;">Titan State and Rodeo Type</mark>

The rodeo damage functions differently, depending on whether you are doomed or not. You'll always take a bar of damage from a battery pull rodeo without any shields. Doomed rodeo will always kill you if you have no shields.

But it gets interesting with the secondary rodeo type, the frag insertion rodeo, where a Pilot with a battery will instead toss a frag inside the battery hatch. This rodeo will deal half a bar of damage to a non-doomed Titan but will kill a doomed Titan if they have no overshield, since it deals damage based on doom bar health.

### <mark style="color:yellow;">Key Features</mark>

* <mark style="color:yellow;">Battery pull rodeo deals 1 bar of damage to non-doomed Titans.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Frag insertion rodeo deals half a bar of damage to non-doomed Titans.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Both rodeos deal damage based on the doom bar health of doomed Titans.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Both rodeos will instakill a doomed Titan that has no overshield.</mark>&#x20;

## <mark style="color:yellow;">Whether Overshield is Present</mark>

Having overshield is essential to not taking any rodeo damage in doom state. But while not doomed, overshield still does matter as a way to reduce or even outright remove damage to your health. Since a battery pull deals a bar of damage, it'll destroy your whole overshield, assuming it's undamaged. Rodeo damage prioritizes damage to the overshield before the HP pool. And if you have too little overshield, the extra damage will transfer to the health pool instead.

Frag insertion rodeo dealing only half a bar of damage to non-doomed Titans is very noticeable. But against a doomed Titan, both rodeo types will deal a doom bar's health worth of damage, it will destroy the overshield if doom bar health is not equal to or is not higher than overshield health. But having even the smallest amount of shields will save you from a death regardless of doom bar health.

### <mark style="color:yellow;">Key Features</mark>

* <mark style="color:yellow;">Overshield is an additional health buffer that will always take damage from rodeo first.</mark>&#x20;
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Spillover extra damage to overshield will damage HP instead.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Battery pull rodeo will deal a bar of damage to non-doomed Titans.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Frag insertion rodeo will deal half a bar of damage to non-doomed Titans.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Both Rodeos deal the equivalent of the doom bar's health.</mark>

## <mark style="color:yellow;">The Amount of HP in Doom State with Overshield</mark>

So the way rodeo damage works in doom state is weird. Depending on how much HP you have while doomed, the enemy rodeo will deal that amount of damage to the overshield, and anything that exceeds the shield HP will be transferred over to the main HP. So a titan that is doomed and gets rodeoed while having shields will lose all shields but lose 0 HP.

Whereas a titan that has only 50% of shields but a full doom bar will lose shields + get damaged for half its doom state HP upon rodeo. And if Titan has, like, 50% HP but full shields, the shields will take 50% of the damage only; nothing will damage the main health so long as shields are higher than health when being rodeoed.

This can be [taken advantage of with Monarch](../monarch/monarch-tech/broken-rodeo-damage-glitch.md) to take barely any damage at all if your doomstate HP is super low and you can use Siphon to gain a bit of shields; this will give you death immunity and make you not even lose the shields, assuming your health is lower than the shields themselves. You won't have less than 50% HP with overshield unless you get shields from energy transfer or energy siphon; a battery will give you 50% doom health and full shields.

This doesn't work on a non-doomed Titan that is close to being doomed and has some overshield; it'll always take a bar or half a bar of damage depending on rodeo type.

### <mark style="color:yellow;">Key Features</mark>

* <mark style="color:yellow;">Having even 1% of overshield with a full doom bar will save you from rodeo death.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Rodeo damage to a doomed Titan is based on the doom bar health, for example if doom bar health is at 50%, it'll deal half a bar's worth of damage to your Titan.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Rodeo damage doesn't account for overshield health, meaning you'll take less damage to the overshield the lower your doom bar health is.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Doesn't work on a non-doomed Titan.</mark>
