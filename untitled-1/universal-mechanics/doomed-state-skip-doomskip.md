# ☣️ Doomed State Skip (Doomskip)

It is not yet clear why it happens, but it seems to require at least two separate forms of damage to happen at the same time in a very strict time window.&#x20;

Here are known recorded Doomskip cases:

* <mark style="color:yellow;">E-Smoke + Leadwall</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">E-Smoke + Thunderbolt</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Plasma Railgun + Leadwall</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">R-97 + Archer</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">XO-16 Chaingun</mark> (surprisingly the only weapon to have done it without a 2nd form of damage)
