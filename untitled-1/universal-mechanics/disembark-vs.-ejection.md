# ℹ️ Disembark vs. Ejection

## <mark style="color:yellow;">**Titan Disembark**</mark>

Exiting your Titan has sometimes some niche and useful applications. Like the ability to reload your gun instantly or the ability to survive a Smart Core confrontation head-on by turning away with your back to the Legion and disembarking right before your Titan dies to survive and run away.\
\
Phase Embark is greatly recommended due to the massive time skip. But unlike with Phase Embark, you actually get a few seconds' delay before being able to re-embark. Without Phase Embark, the Pilot is nearly unkillable when he disembarks and instantly re-embarks to pick up a battery; thus, it's better to just go straight for the Titan and not the Pilot.

### <mark style="color:yellow;">Benefits From Disembarking</mark>

* <mark style="color:yellow;">Reload your weapon instantly</mark> (Ion's energy too).
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Refresh your ability instantly or reduce the cooldown by a lot</mark> **(**&#x65;xample: Gun Shield, Amped Wall).
* <mark style="color:yellow;">A survivable escape from smart core confrontation</mark> (face the Legion backwards and disembark right before your Titan dies and flee without Legion locking onto you).

## <mark style="color:yellow;">**Titan Eject**</mark>

Ejecting has its benefits too. You can take advantage of the ability to destroy your Titan earlier if you're in a non survivable situation, like getting trapped and massacred by several Titans at once. Ejecting earlier, especially right before getting into doomstate can greatly reduce enemy Titans' core gain. If a Titan dooms you, they get 20% core for free!

### <mark style="color:yellow;">Benefits from Ejecting</mark>

* <mark style="color:yellow;">Early eject to reduce enemies' ability to farm you for core</mark> (getting doomed alone grants the enemy a free 10% core).
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Class-specific eject.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Deal eject damage to enemy Titan.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Abuse of damage immunity and continued weapon firing</mark> (ejecting while laser-coring, for example).



## <mark style="color:yellow;">**Class-Specific Eject**</mark>

### <mark style="color:yellow;">You can make ejection safer by using</mark>

<mark style="color:yellow;">Holo Pilot + Pilot Hover:</mark> Using Holo as soon as you eject and then instantly hovering allows you to dodge the enemy. The hologram will maintain your momentum and keep on flying upwards while you hang behind. You don't have to use the entirety of hover; you can save some to use later up in the air to dodge Railgun shots or Laser Shots by hovering right before you think they'll fire for them to possibly miss.

<mark style="color:yellow;">Grapple Pilot:</mark> Grappling the floor as soon as you eject, not only will you quickly fly back down to the ground, but your hitbox will also desync making it a lot harder to get killed midair.

<mark style="color:yellow;">Stim Pilot:</mark> Stimming as soon as you eject will greatly increase your eject velocity. Allowing you to strafe in midair (hold the left or right direction key and move your mouse to strafe), making it very difficult to shoot you down without a Laser Shot or Charge Rifle.

<mark style="color:yellow;">Phase Pilot:</mark> Phasing midair (not necessarily right away unless it's a Legion with a Power Shot/Smart Core ready) will allow you to survive anything briefly, including a nuke in a closed area.

<mark style="color:yellow;">Cloak Pilot + Low Profile:</mark> Cloaking with the Low Profile kit makes you visually nearly impossible to hit and also prevents Smart Core from locking onto you.&#x20;

## <mark style="color:yellow;">Damage Immunity and Continued Weapon Firing Eject</mark>

Ejecting deals 1,800 damage, but also some weapons will keep on firing for a few seconds after ejecting. You can utilize ejection immunity and explosion damage to your advantage. Basically, depending on the weapon, whenever you eject mid-fire, the weapon fire animation will keep on playing for longer until the Titan explodes fully.&#x20;

For example, you dash, melee, and press eject as soon as you press the melee. The enemy Titan will try to execute you, but you're immune to damage. Your melee will hit them for full damage, and, moreover, your ejection damage will deal 1,800 titan damage to them too. You can win an LTS round by ejection damage, not by nuke ejection though; the round will end before the nuke activates.&#x20;

### <mark style="color:yellow;">List of Weapons that will Continue Firing after Ejecting</mark>

* <mark style="color:yellow;">Melee, Arc Wave, Firewall.</mark>&#x20;
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Tone Tracker missiles, Monarch Rocket Salvo, Missile Rack, Multi-Target Missile System (MTMS), Cluster Missile.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Flame Core, Salvo Core, Laser Core, Sword Core melee & Arc Wave.</mark>&#x20;
