# 🟢 Auto Titan Tricks

## <mark style="color:yellow;">**Auto-Titan Animation Break**</mark>

Auto-Titans, by default, will try to evade any kind of AoE (Area of Effect) damage sources, like e-smoke, thermite, etc., by either running away or making giant unnatural leaps. Unfortunately, even friendly AoE damage sources can cause the AI to do so. Hence, it can result in annoyance when trying to pull a battery as a Pilot only to see your Titan leap away from you because of a nearby Cluster Missile. \
\
Auto-Titans can also detect enemy Titans nearby by sound, and if they hear one approaching, they'll sometimes leap/move outside of cover to engage, resulting in your Titan being farmed by the enemy Titan for damage.\
\
A solution to those problems is the Animation Break bug; whenever you switch your Titan from guard mode to follow mode, and vice versa, the Titan's current animation will stop entirely to readjust function.

### <mark style="color:yellow;">Use Cases</mark>

* <mark style="color:yellow;">Nearly entirely stop the Auto-Titan from moving.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Greatly lower or even outright entirely stop the fire rate.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">To prevent reload.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Tap once to stop the current melee attack animation.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Tap once to make Auto-Titan recover instantly from stagger animation when heavily damaged.</mark>
