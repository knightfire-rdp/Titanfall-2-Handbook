# 🚧 Amped Titan Weapons/Abilities

## <mark style="color:yellow;">You Can Amp Titan Weapons and Abilities?</mark>

Yes, you can amp some Titan weapons and abilities, and the amps are all different from each other. We got as low as a 15% damage increase on Laser Shot, all the way up to a goddamn 250% damage increase on Leadwall!

Is this practical in a fight? No, but is it fun, and can you do silly stuff like [amped-leadwall.md](../titan-competitive-stuff/questionable-strategies/amped-leadwall.md "mention")? YES.
