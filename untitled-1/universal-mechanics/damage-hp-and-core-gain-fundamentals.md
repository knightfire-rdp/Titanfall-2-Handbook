# ℹ️ Damage, HP, and Core Gain Fundamentals

## <mark style="color:purple;">**Damage Types**</mark>

Every weapon/ability/core uses 2 specific values for 2 different damage types, Titan and Entity.

Titan damage is usually applied to large entities like Titans, Reapers, and drop-ships. Additionally, the Titan overshield too, despite being able to be shot by a Pilot weapon from anywhere.

Entity damage is typically applied to small entities like Pilots, Spectres, and Stalkers. It is also applied to the solid defensive abilities of Titans and Pilots alike (Particle Wall, Gun Shield, Tether Trap, A-Wall, etc.).

All weapons' Titan damage either scales higher than Entity damage or is kept the same, but normally Titan damage is way higher than Entity damage. Easy to figure out by shooting a Particle Wall or Gun Shield, since those have Entity health.

<mark style="color:yellow;">Low Entity Damage,</mark> <mark style="color:red;">High Titan Damage</mark>

* <mark style="color:yellow;">EPG = 100 :</mark> <mark style="color:red;">700</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Archer =</mark> <mark style="color:yellow;">100 :</mark> <mark style="color:red;">2,250</mark>

<mark style="color:yellow;">Same Entity and Titan Damage</mark>

* <mark style="color:yellow;">Mozambique =</mark> <mark style="color:yellow;">30 :</mark> <mark style="color:yellow;">30</mark>

* <mark style="color:yellow;">Ronin Sword Melee = 800 :</mark> <mark style="color:yellow;">800</mark>



## <mark style="color:red;">**Titan HP**</mark>

1 bar represents 2,500 HP, with each Titan chassis having differing bar counts.

#### <mark style="color:yellow;">For reference, Titan chassis total hp values</mark>

* <mark style="color:yellow;">Stryder:</mark> <mark style="color:red;">3 bars</mark> (7,500 HP)
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Atlas:</mark> <mark style="color:red;">4 bars</mark> (10,000 HP)
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Ogre:</mark> <mark style="color:red;">5 bars</mark> (12,500 HP)
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Monarch Superior Chassis:</mark> <mark style="color:red;">6 bars</mark> (15,000 HP)<br>

<mark style="color:yellow;">Excludes doomstate</mark> (2,500 HP)<mark style="color:yellow;">, overshield</mark> (2,500 HP)<mark style="color:yellow;">, and Aegis HP upgrade</mark> (2,500 HP).



## <mark style="color:blue;">**Titan Overshield (Energy Shield)**</mark>&#x20;

One singular bar represents 2,500 Titan HP (3,750 with Aegis), which gets fully restored upon receiving a battery or using Monarch's Upgrade Core.&#x20;

#### <mark style="color:yellow;">Additional info</mark>

* <mark style="color:yellow;">Titan shields don't have a weak spot for crits but can still be hit from anywhere by Pilot weapons.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">You will not receive any core from losing shield HP, excluding with the Aegis Ronin Sword Block upgrade.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Energy Siphon and Arc Grenade take away half of the overshield per attack</mark> (even through Sword Block)<mark style="color:yellow;">.</mark>
* [So long as you have any energy shield left, a Pilot rodeo will not kill your doomed state Titan.](../monarch/monarch-tech/broken-rodeo-damage-glitch.md)
* <mark style="color:yellow;">A rodeoing Pilot will be immune to damage when rodeoing your Titan, assuming you have an overshield, even against explosive damage; there are only few exceptions.</mark>



## <mark style="color:yellow;">**Titan Doomstate**</mark>

One singular bar of 2,500 Titan HP that gets partially repaired from taking a battery; in this state you are prone to getting executed by a Titan melee. Unless you activate the eject sequence before the melee hits you. Or you have less HP than the Titan melee's damage.<br>

## <mark style="color:orange;">**Core Gain**</mark>

Each damage dealt to a “Titan” and received as a Titan gains you core. Of course, there are more ways to earn core, and it depends on the game mode. Whether in Titan or Pilot mode, there will be a change in core gain from kills.&#x20;
