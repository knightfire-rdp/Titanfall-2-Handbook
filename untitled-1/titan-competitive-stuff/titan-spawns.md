# 🔄 Titan Spawns

Titan spawns in Last Titan Standing are a lot more confusing than you may think. Whether you are on the team that the host started the lobby on, when you join the lobby, when someone leaves, etc. Several factors determine the spawns; said factors are in a chain in order, so if factor 2 changes, then that affects factors 3-7 too, but not factor 1, since factor 1 already changes the factors after.&#x20;

Because the Handbook is made with the competitive scene in mind, aka. LTS PuGs, there is a team limit of 5 players, so all the spawns account for a max of 5v5 teamfights. Spawns change a lot with more players. &#x20;

## <mark style="color:yellow;">Factors That Affect Spawns in Order from 1-7</mark>

1. <mark style="color:yellow;">Host side vs. non-host side.</mark> \
   The team the host was on at the start of the lobby will be considered the host side, meaning the 2 teams have fixed sides on which sides they'll spawn. If the host switches teams, he'll no longer be on the host side.
2. <mark style="color:yellow;">When you join the lobby.</mark>\
   The player spawns within a team are influenced by when a player joins the lobby; the first player to join the lobby has the 1st spawn, the 2nd player has the 2nd, and so on. So if the host makes the lobby and is on team 1, and then a 2nd player joins and goes on team 2. The player from team 1 will have the 1st player spawn if they switch to team 2 because they were in the lobby before the other player.
3. <mark style="color:yellow;">When a person leaves, your position shifts.</mark>\
   For example, if there are 3 players on the non-host side team and player 2 leaves. Then player 3 will become player 2.
4. <mark style="color:yellow;">If a person joins back, they regain their lost position.</mark>\
   For example, after player 2 leaves, then player 3 becomes player 2. If player 2, that left the lobby joins back, then he will regain his player 2 status, shifting player 3 back to his player 3 status.
5. <mark style="color:yellow;">If more than one person leaves.</mark>\
   For example, there are 4 players on a team, and players 2 and 3 leave, leaving players 1 and 4 in the lobby. Player 4 will become player 2, and if any of those 2 players who left come back, they'll have to get new player positions. So the next to join will become player 3 and the next after will become player 4.
6. <mark style="color:yellow;">Some maps change the spawns of a few players once before the side switch.</mark>\*\
   Basically few players on a team on the host side, non-host side, or heck, even on both sides will swap places. The swaps will happen once after one round on a side on the maps Eden, Drydock, Crash Site, Complex, Colony, and War Games.\
   <mark style="background-color:$primary;">War Games is the only map where a player changes their spawn after a round instead of switching with a player.</mark>&#x20;
7. <mark style="color:yellow;">Sides switch once after two rounds.</mark>\
   The host and non-host spawn sides switch once after 2 rounds. Do keep in mind factor 6 spawn swapping between teammates after one round on some maps.

{% hint style="info" %}
\*<mark style="color:yellow;">Like in factor 6. The map sides marked with the “</mark>:arrows\_counterclockwise:<mark style="color:yellow;">” symbol switches teammate spawns between eachother after one round. They reset to previous spawns after the sides. The spawns will then switch again on round 4 and persist till the match ends.</mark>&#x20;
{% endhint %}



## <mark style="color:yellow;">List of Host vs. Non-Host Spawn Sides</mark>

1. <mark style="color:yellow;">Boomtown</mark>
   * <mark style="color:$info;">Host:</mark> Large building side, need stim for battery.
   * <mark style="color:$info;">Non-Host:</mark> Grandma's house side, pull with amped wall.
2. <mark style="color:yellow;">Exoplanet</mark>
   * <mark style="color:$info;">Host:</mark> Silos side, no fast pull.
   * <mark style="color:$info;">Non-Host:</mark> Fast pull side.
3. <mark style="color:yellow;">Eden</mark>
   * <mark style="color:$info;">Host:</mark> Fields/Dropship/Coffee side.
   * <mark style="color:$info;">Non-Host:</mark> Slums side. :arrows\_counterclockwise:
4. <mark style="color:yellow;">Drydock</mark>
   * <mark style="color:$info;">Host:</mark> Fast pull mid side. :arrows\_counterclockwise:
   * <mark style="color:$info;">Non-Host:</mark> Crane spawn side. :arrows\_counterclockwise:
5. <mark style="color:yellow;">Crash Site</mark>
   * <mark style="color:$info;">Host:</mark> Bottom slow wide hill push side. :arrows\_counterclockwise:
   * <mark style="color:$info;">Non-Host:</mark> Fast narrow hill push side. :arrows\_counterclockwise:
6. <mark style="color:yellow;">Complex</mark>
   * <mark style="color:$info;">Host:</mark> Outside spawn, dome side, top of staircase.&#x20;
   * <mark style="color:$info;">Non-Host:</mark> Inside spawn, at the bottom of the staircase. :arrows\_counterclockwise:
7. <mark style="color:yellow;">Angel City</mark>
   * <mark style="color:$info;">Host:</mark> Field on the left side of the spawn side.
   * <mark style="color:$info;">Non-Host:</mark> Field on the right side of the spawn side.
8. <mark style="color:yellow;">Colony</mark>
   * <mark style="color:$info;">Host:</mark> Hill on the left side of the spawn side. :arrows\_counterclockwise:
   * <mark style="color:$info;">Non-Host:</mark> Hill on the right side of the spawn side.
9. <mark style="color:yellow;">Glitch</mark>
   * <mark style="color:$info;">Host:</mark> Orange battery side.
   * <mark style="color:$info;">Non-Host:</mark> Blue battery side.
10. <mark style="color:yellow;">War Games</mark>

    * <mark style="color:$info;">Host:</mark> Pit/Dropship side.
    * <mark style="color:$info;">Non-Host:</mark> Fast pull side. :arrows\_counterclockwise:
