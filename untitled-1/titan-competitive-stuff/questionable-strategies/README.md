# 🧠 Questionable Strategies

Questionable Strategies is all about the weirdest kinds of strategies that CAN work but are generally very silly and memey. A prime example being [r-97-vortex-gimmick.md](r-97-vortex-gimmick.md "mention") where you use the R-97 SMG to feed Ion's Vortex and reflex 31 bullets for max damage rapidly. It's silly and memey because any smart player can just snipe the pilot or push in with Ion or Ronin; it's very situational.

All of them are allowed in competitive play of Last Titan Standing, except for [kamikaze-ronin.md](kamikaze-ronin.md "mention") because it requires satchels. Since it's the only strategy that involves satchels, I'll make an exception. I'll let it stay here in this category despite not being allowed in competitive play.
