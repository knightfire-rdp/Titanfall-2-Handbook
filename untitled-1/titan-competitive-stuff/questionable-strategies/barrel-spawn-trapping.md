# 🛢️💥Barrel Spawn Trapping

Blue barrels are things that even the best Ronin mains fear. Countless have lost their lives to such puny and small objects. They're known to kill Ronins that dare to phase inside of them. Thankfully such powerful beings exist only on Drydock and Crash Site.&#x20;

The cool thing about the blue barrels is that they're actual movable solid entities that, when you phase inside of them, will one-shot your Titan. I assume the game checks for whether your phase can kill the entity or not. If it can't kill the entity, the Ronin will explode. Using Sword Block normally lets you often times block the phase damage when phasing into Reapers or Titans; <mark style="color:red;">I'm not sure about barrels</mark>.

Many don't know this, but the barrels are the only things that don't disappear or get reset back to position after a round of LTS ends. So in theory it's possible to move the blue barrels to where a Ronin might use Phase Dash to kill them. Heck, even place them near enemy spawn, where Ronins predictably spam Phase Dash off spawn to reach batteries faster. Combine that knowledge with [titan-spawns.md](../titan-spawns.md "mention") and you can strategize in some silly way, shenanigans. Pilots can move barrels too, but a lot worse.
