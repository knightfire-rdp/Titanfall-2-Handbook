# 🛡️🔫R-97 Vortex Gimmick

Same as [Power Shot upcycling](../../ion/ion-tech/power-shot-upcycle-and-salvage-with-vortex.md), Vortex Shield can also convert R-97 bullets to deal 140 damage per bullet, or 4,340 damage for the whole shield. With amped Vortex Shield making each bullet deal 189 damage, or 5,859 damage for a full shield.

Why R-97, though? Well, it's the fastest-firing weapon in the game; it can easily fire those 31 bullets in a second. You can fill and fire the Vortex Shield like 4 times in a row before it runs out of energy due to its speed.

## <mark style="color:yellow;">Distant Filling</mark>

The safe and good strategy is to fill from a distance as a Pilot. Pilot hiding somewhere high or somewhere where Ion can face both the enemy Titan and the Pilot without risking taking damage. This strat is way safer for the Pilot but also can be a lot harder to fill Ion when she's distracted by a Titan. &#x20;

## <mark style="color:yellow;">Rodeo Filling</mark>

The even cooler part is, though, that you can fill the Ion while rodeoing. If the Ion looks up while vortex-shielding and you fire upwards, you'll be able to fire directly into the Vortex Shield. Instant filler on demand, but make sure to stand on a flat surface, or you won't be able to catch the bullets.

Auto-Scorch with Assault Chip and Wildfire kit is recommended for this because he's the most dangerous Auto-Titan by far, especially in close range where enemies will try to get close to melee the Pilot.

But yeah, the strongest counter to this strat is an enemy Ion catching the bullets; it can easily backfire.

### <mark style="color:yellow;">**Rodeoing Pilot is Liable to the Following Things**</mark>

* <mark style="color:yellow;">Enemy Vortex Shield reflecting the bullets.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Titan or Pilot Melee.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Any kind of explosive damage, including 40 mm Tracker Cannon.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Walking into a Tripwire will instantly kill the Pilot.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Ronin phasing into the Ion, killing the Pilot.</mark>
