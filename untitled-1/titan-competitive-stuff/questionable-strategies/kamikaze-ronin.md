# ⚔️💀Kamikaze Ronin

<mark style="color:red;">This is not allowed in competitive play because of Satchels.</mark>

Yea, so this is a thing that I enjoyed doing in public games. Have one player play Ronin, and have others use an A-Wall and Satchels to turn you into a walking bomb. The moment an enemy attack hits one of the satchels, they'll all be converted into enemy Satchels and explode together in a chain reaction. Ronin is required for this because he has Phase Dash to close in on the enemies before his satchels are triggered and because he has Sword Block to negate most of the damage.

It's a very risky but funny strategy since you must get right in the face of an enemy. It won't let you get core nor hit multiple enemies because when they get converted, they won't deal friendly damage, but only damage to the enemy and to the owner. So when an enemy hits a Satchel and it explodes, it'll cause other Satchels to explode too. They'll get core from the damage. But they'll take a ton of self-damage too. So the idea here is to doom them and rip them. God forbid they hit your Satchels while you're near your teammates; all of you will get damaged.

From practice, it's best to strap the Satchels onto Ronin's feet and legs because Tone's 40 mm splash will hit them easily, even if you plant them onto Ronin's back. You want to face-hug the enemy and force them to hit your Satchels; the enemy dropping electric smoke is the easiest way to do it.

40 mm cannon, e-smoke, Tripwire, Cluster Missile, and Flame Shield. They are all powerful at activating the satchels prematurely.

### <mark style="color:yellow;">Important Things to Note</mark>

* <mark style="color:yellow;">Satchels hit by the enemy will become the enemy's Satchels and explode, dealing damage to the enemy, to you, and to your teammates.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">The enemy will get core from the Satchels' damage.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Ronin can easily get close to enemies and can reduce Satchel damage.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Amp Satchels for 50% more damage.</mark>
