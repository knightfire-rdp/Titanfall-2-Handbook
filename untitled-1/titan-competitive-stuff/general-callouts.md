# 🗣️ General Callouts

This is a page that serves as a callout template for the competitive scene in the last Titan Standing PUGs (pickup games) community. You can always make your own callouts or get more creative and use the ones listed and expand and make better callouts that suit your team's playstyle more.

Callouts are very undervalued in the comp scene; this page will cover basic rules that I've personally developed from playing years of pugs. I'll list the callouts in order of importance from my opinion.

For reference, "comms" = "communication."

## <mark style="color:yellow;">Absolute Beginner Callouts</mark>

These are generally the most basic callouts that you can learn to use in PuGs:

“(Titan name) is in/on the left/right side” (where the enemy is; can also say “in mid/middle”, in the middle/middle lane).&#x20;

“(Titan name) is on (Landmark)” (ask others during games what to call those landmarks; a good example could be "rubble," the area uphill behind the bridge, on Boomtown).

“(Titan name) is 1/2/3/4/5 bars” (how much health the enemy currently has).

"(Titan name) is doomed" (always useful to call out).

“(Titan name) has (Titan Core, for example "Laser Core")”





## <mark style="color:yellow;">Rules for Good Callouts</mark>

* <mark style="color:yellow;">Keep chitchat to a minimum</mark> (rounds are short and so is the time; unnecessary clutter of communication can severely impact the team's ability to coordinate)<mark style="color:yellow;">.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Don't flood the communication with information</mark> (even if using the right callouts, too many callouts from a single person, especially in 3v3 and above, can lead to cluttered comms)<mark style="color:yellow;">.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Use callouts in a way that you'd want the teammates to give callouts to you</mark> (you'll learn with time what callouts are useful for your team and which are unnecessary; your team doesn't always need to know “every single” action you do)<mark style="color:yellow;">.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Don't let emotions affect your callouts</mark> (for example, when you die, don't talk about how you messed up and should've done x to beat x or how you're frustrated at yourself for playing badly; just stop and do it after the round ends).&#x20;
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Learn the importance of callout types and when to use them</mark> (for example, often times saying “I hit laser shot on Ion” has a lot less meaning than saying “Ion is 3 bars” or “punished Ion for a bar”)<mark style="color:yellow;">.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">The 4 main callout types</mark> (enemy Titans' cores, positions, health, and current resources, like abilities or ammo)<mark style="color:yellow;">.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">The tone of your callouts and repeating important callouts.</mark> (As mentioned later by <mark style="color:yellow;">Teammate/You Targeted by Enemy</mark> callouts, it's a good idea to change the tone of callouts based on urgency, like when calling out your teammates by name; it's also a good idea to repeat urgent callouts up to 3 times.)
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Optional callouts</mark> (like your position, setting up a push, distracting an enemy, needing assistance, etc.)<mark style="color:yellow;">.</mark>





## <mark style="color:yellow;">Enemy Callouts Importance</mark>&#x20;

Generally speaking, the most essential callouts towards the enemy are enemy Titan cores, followed by enemy Titan positions, enemy Titans' resources, and finally, enemy HP status. \
For example, “Tone and Ion on hill (colony map callout),” optionally followed by “Tone's wall is gone” or “Tone is 2 bars.” Ofc, there can be rules around it, like ability callouts only in proximity to teammates, which then allows you to prioritize enemy HP over abilities.

You can also call out uncommon enemy Titan kits, like if you see Ion drop tripwire while holding laser shot, it means she doesn't have the zero point tripwire kit and instead has something else; she's doing the [ZPL](../ion/ion-tech/zero-point-laser-shot-zpl.md) glitch. Or some kits are very obvious, like Tone's Double Sonar or Reinforced Wall. Normally, people run Zero Point Tripwire, Inferno Shield, Viper Thrusters, Thunderstorm, Rocket Barrage, Hidden Compartment, and Rapid Rearm.

<mark style="color:yellow;">Callouts' importance prioritizes Ronin;</mark> Ronin should always be called out the most since he's the most dangerous and punishing Titan (for example, he flanks a reloading Legion, deals 3 bars of damage, and runs away unscathed). That's where callouts like “hit Power-Shot/Laser-Shot on Ronin” matter, since hitting strong hits through Sword Block is a game changer, and Ronin should always be prioritized callout-wise.

1. <mark style="color:yellow;">Enemy Titan Cores</mark> (ALWAYS call out cores; if the enemy got a core or the enemy is coring, call it out, since it's a game changer; even if you're in a dome fighting an Ion for example, that Ion could direct the Laser Core at your teammates in mid-fight)<mark style="color:yellow;">.</mark>
2. <mark style="color:yellow;">Enemy Titan Positions</mark> (preferably switch to resource callouts if enemies are near teammates)<mark style="color:yellow;">.</mark>
3. <mark style="color:yellow;">Enemy Titan Resources</mark> (optionally, if no teammate is near, could do HP callouts instead).
4. <mark style="color:yellow;">Enemy Titan HP Status</mark> (can increase importance if enemy is close to getting doomed, got doomed, or teammate asks for HP status OR on enemy Ronin)

### <mark style="color:yellow;">Enemy Callout Examples</mark>

X stands for an enemy Titan of any kind\
burned = wasted\
batt = battery\
trips = Ion's tripwire

<mark style="color:yellow;">Enemy Titan Cores</mark>

* “X has a core.”
* “X is coring!”
* “X used/burned core.”
* “X is close to core.” (He damaged someone heavily.)
* “Salvo Core is in the air.” (Tone can hold the Salvo Core for up to 10 sec in the air.)
* “Salvo Core hit a building/x.” / “Salvo Core is gone.”



<mark style="color:yellow;">Enemy Titan Positions</mark>

* “X is left/mid/right.”
* “X and X are in mid.”
* “X is moving to Dome.” (Complex map callout.)
* “X is pulling left batt/battery.”



<mark style="color:yellow;">Enemy Titan Resources</mark>

* “Ion low on energy.”
* “Watch trips.” or “Ion placed Tripwire.”&#x20;
* “No trips.” or “Tripwire is gone.”
* “Ion doesn't have a Zero Point Tripwire.”
* “Ion is anchoring.” / “Ion anchor.” (Shielding enemy battery pull.)



* “Scorch placed/burned both canisters.” (Rarely needed to call out.)
* “Scorch is low on fuel.” (Saw them use it up on blocking or burning a Titan.)
* “Scorch used/burned Firewall.” (Rarely needed to call out.)
* “Scorch is anchoring.” or “Scorch anchor.” (Shielding enemy battery pull.)



* “Northstar hovering.”
* “Northstar used/burned Cluster Missile.”



* “Ronin used/burned Arc Wave/both Arc Waves.”
* “Ronin used/out of phase.”
* “Ronin out of ammo.” or “No Leadwall.” (He has to unblock to reload; you can pressure him.)



* “Tone has locks on me.” (Useful if Ion teammates are beside you.)
* “Tone placed Particle Wall.”
* “Wall is down.”
* “Tone has a Reinforced Particle Wall.” (Useful for teammates to know.)
* “Tone has Pulse Echo/Double Sonar”
* “Tone is anchoring.” / “Tone anchor.” (Shielding enemy battery pull.)



* “Gun Shield down.”
* “Broke/Destroyed Gun Shield.” (With Arc Wave/Railgun/Tone Missiles.)
* “Legion used/burned Power Shot/both Power Shots.”
* “Legion is anchoring.” or “Legion anchor.” (Shielding enemy battery pull.)
* “Legion is out of ammo.” (Longest reload in the game is 4 seconds, and Legion is literally harmless without ammo.)
* “Legion is low on ammo.” (Audible and opens up a possibility for a big punishment due to a slow reload.)
* “Legion in long-range mode.” (If he fired for a bit, he'll definitely be low on ammo and need to switch modes when getting pushed; it's easy to tell the mode by the red bullet trail.)



* “Monarch used Siphon/both Siphons.”
* “Monarch used Rearm.” (She won't be able to quadruple dash for an easy escape.)



<mark style="color:yellow;">Enemy Titan HP Status</mark> (most important on Ronin)

* “X is a/one bar.”
* “X is 2/3/4/5 bars.”
* “X lost shields.” (This is only necessary on Ronin due to how tanky he is.)
* “X is one shot to doom.” (Just very close to being doomed.)
* “X is doomed.”
* “X is half a doom bar.” (or 1 third doom bar, etc.)
* “X is one shot” (Literally super low health while doomed.)
* “X is Power Shot/Laser Shot/Sword Core melee range.” (Basically one shot via that ability.)





## <mark style="color:yellow;">Teammate/Self Callouts Importance</mark>

The importance of them is generally not as important as enemy callouts and should NOT fully replace the enemy callouts; they should be used more sparingly when the opportunity is right for the situation. \
But in general, the most important callouts are towards teammates being targeted by an enemy Titan, you having your teammate's back or being in position to push, and your resources. You are welcome to use them how you want, though; they're not as importance-heavy as enemy callouts in which order you use them.

For example, “X, Ronin coming to you,” “I'm behind you, X,” and “I'm low on energy”

1. <mark style="color:yellow;">Teammate/You Targeted by Enemy</mark>&#x20;
   1. (Be it a Northstar watching the battery your teammate wants to pull, or Ronin moving to a teammate to attack them from behind, or Ion blocking you from helping your teammates as Legion, they're important.)
2. <mark style="color:yellow;">Being in Position to Assist Your Teammate</mark>&#x20;
   1. (like telling them you're behind them or are distracting an enemy Titan for your teammate to punish from behind, anchoring a battery pull for them, etc.)
3. <mark style="color:yellow;">Your Resources</mark>&#x20;
   1. (Like saying if you're close to or have core, whether your energy is back or your offensive/defensive abilities are ready to engage or assist in something.)

### <mark style="color:yellow;">Teammate/Self Callout Examples</mark>

X stands for an enemy Titan of any kind\
batt = battery

<mark style="color:yellow;">Teammate/You Targeted by Enemy</mark>

* “X is watching batt/battery.”
* “(Player Name), X is behind you.” (Can call the teammate by their name to quickly grab their attention, names help grab attention the best in urgent situations.)
* “(Player Name), X is rotating to you from mid.” (Any location works, can be more helpful than saying simply from behind.)
* “Ion is blocking me.” or “Ion is in my face.” (Especially common on Legion, where Ion can completely prevent you from helping your teammates.)

<mark style="color:yellow;">Being in a Position to Assist Your Teammates</mark>

* “(Player name), I'm behind you.”
* “(Player name), I'm watching you.”
* “X is watching me.” or “X is distracted by me.”
* “Blocking X off.” (For example, Legion fully blocks Northstar from shooting teammates, can't even get past him.)
* “I can anchor for you.” or “I'm anchoring.” or “Can you anchor?” or “Anchor me.”
* “Let's switch.” (You go for x, while they go for x, useful if Ion is low on energy or to suit counters; for example, you can send Ronin to finish off low-energy Ion while you fight Ronin.)
* “Line of sight broken by e-smoke.”&#x20;

<mark style="color:yellow;">Your Resources</mark>

* “I have no energy/wall/gun-shield/fuel.” (If a teammate asks you to anchor and you can't.)
* “Reloading.” or “I'm reloading.” (Literally only for Legion because of slow reload.)





## <mark style="color:yellow;">Optional Callouts</mark>

Aside from the main ones, optional callouts exist too, but those are usually unique to each player or team if you have a team or players you play regularly with. You could make custom callouts like, for example, saying “Power Shot ready.” and have your Ronin tell you when to Power Shot at him when he's behind e-smoke meleeing enemy Ronin. Can't really give any example since that's more of an advanced side, which everyone should try to create themselves to suit their and teammates' needs.&#x20;
