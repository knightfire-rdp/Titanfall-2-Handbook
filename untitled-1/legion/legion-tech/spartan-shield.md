# 🟢 Spartan Shield

<mark style="color:yellow;">**You can sprint with your Gun Shield when you embark while holding the Gun Shield key.**</mark> That effect is a one-time thing, though; you'll need to perform the glitch again for the next Gun Shield. It's called Spartan Shield because it feels familiar to the Hoplon shields used by Spartans when charging at the enemies.

When you try to fire during the effect, you'll have to spool in first but can then go back to sprinting afterward. Similar case to reload and gun mode switch, but you'll be forced into [walking speed](full-gun-shield-movement.md) when doing either of those until you're done.

Power-shotting at any point during Spartan Shield will cause it to break and become the normal Gun Shield, meaning you'll be unable to sprint.

This glitch has some very niche use cases, like sprinting through a short corridor while protected against Northstar shots or even pushing towards an enemy Tone. The Gun Shield is very strange during the sprint animation; its hitbox lowers down really far down to the ground, meaning you're a lot more covered than you think.

#### <mark style="color:yellow;">Key Features</mark>

* <mark style="color:yellow;">Ability to sprint with your Gun Shield once.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Name based on Spartan Hoplon shields.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Can fire</mark> <mark style="color:yellow;"></mark><mark style="color:yellow;">during the glitch but needs to spool in first.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Can reload and switch gun modes during the glitch but will be slowed down to walking speed.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Ability to push enemy gunfire to some extent.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">The Gun Shield has a much bigger hitbox from below than visually shown.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Power-shotting breaks the Spartan Shield, turning your Gun Shield back to normal.</mark>
