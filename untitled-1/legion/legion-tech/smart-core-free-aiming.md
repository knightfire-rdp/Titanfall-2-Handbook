# 🟢 Smart Core Free-Aiming

## <mark style="color:yellow;">Smart Core's Big Problem</mark>

Smart Core requires a direct line of sight to lock onto targets, but it actually aims for center mass. So if a target, heck even Pilot, hides behind some cover at least to the height of their chest, they'll be fully immune to the Smart Core.&#x20;

Legion shouts that he's activating Smart Core, even showing up in subtitles. Giving enemies the time to back out or use cover to evade it. So rest assured you'll have enemies prepared to manipulate Smart Core in their favor.

Sure you can still power-shot them like normal, but the lock-on will force your bullets to miss the target and instead hit the cover. This becomes most apparent at Crash Site on top of the hill where smart players will take advantage of this disability and hide behind the crate making Smart Core an actual liability.

There is also the issue that when players disembark, Smart Core will literally start missing the Auto-Titan for several seconds after they disembark. Which is actually absurd, even if that happens right in front of you. Thankfully there are multiple solutions for those problems.



## <mark style="color:yellow;">The 2 Free Aim Methods</mark>

With many bad things there are also great solutions. Here are 2 main useful methods to free aim with Smart Core to get past the commonly faced difficulties. Keep in mind that free aiming means you'll have the Predator Cannon fire spread like without Smart Core, so it might be a good idea to use long-range mode when free-aiming Smart Core.



### <mark style="color:yellow;">Blinding Yourself with Electric Smoke</mark>

Electric Smoke actually can block your line of sight with the opponents quite literally. Deploy the E-Smoke and take a step or more backward; you'll be blind but be able to free-aim the Predator Cannon. It even works with enemies's and teammates' E-Smoke as well.

### <mark style="color:yellow;">Being Out of Detection Range</mark>

Smart Core has a max range up to which it can lock onto targets. This max range is the same for close-range and long-range modes. But it's generally out of effective close-range Power Shot range, so it's best to use it in long-range mode.
