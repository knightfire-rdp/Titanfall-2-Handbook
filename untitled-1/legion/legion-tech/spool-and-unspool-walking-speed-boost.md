# 🟢 Spool & Unspool Walking Speed Boost

You know how Legion has a movement speed penalty when aiming down his Predator Cannon? You didn't know, well neither did I a few years ago. But besides that, the Light Weight Alloy kit lets you reduce that penalty letting you walk at 75% movement walking speed. That is not enough….

You can actually get a full 100% walking movement speed by constantly aiming and unaiming the gun. Without the LWA kit you'll need to keep your gun unspooled at like 70% of full aim for maximum benefit. With the LWA kit you only need to keep it at like 90% or so.

Unspooling and spooling is useful for when you want to walk noticably faster while being able to do a Quickdraw Power Shot quickly at any moment, also to be ready to fire nearly instantly should something show up. Do keep in mind this only works when you're not using any ability that would force you to be in aim mode or fully out of it.

### <mark style="color:yellow;">Key Features</mark>

* <mark style="color:yellow;">Ability to walk at 100% movement speed without the 55% movement speed penalty.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Spooling and unspooling your gun lets you overcome that penalty.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">By default you should aim for around 70% spooled in the gun.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">With the Light Weight Alloy kit you can aim for around 90% spooled in the gun.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Doesn't work with abilities since they force you out or force you into aim.</mark>
* [Full Gun Shield Movement](full-gun-shield-movement.md) <mark style="color:yellow;">tech can be used instead with Gun Shield.</mark>
