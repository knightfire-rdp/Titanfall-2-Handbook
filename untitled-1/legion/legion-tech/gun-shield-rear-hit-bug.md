# 🔻 Gun Shield Rear Hit Bug

This is one strange and annoying bug that's exclusive to Legion. Basically, you're unable to shoot projectiles from behind a friendly/enemy Legion's Gun Shield; they just end up hitting it instead, or in the case of throwables (like Tripwire and Tether Traps), the projectile will bounce against it. Hitscan[^1] weapons, on the other hand, work from behind with no problem.

The stranger part is that this isn't the case with Ion, Scorch, and Tone. Like shooting from behind Vortex Shield, Thermal Shield, and Particle Wall. Those work fine with projectiles from behind.&#x20;

* Salvo Core.

<mark style="color:yellow;">Legion</mark>

* Long-Range Power Shot.

<mark style="color:yellow;">Monarch</mark>
