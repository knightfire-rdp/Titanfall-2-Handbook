# 🟢 Sword Core Dash Unlock

Normally when trying to [dash after a melee, you'll exhaust your dash](../../universal-mechanics/melee-tech.md#melee-dash-penalty) unless you wait out a bit. On Ronin, though, you can fully prevent it if you activate Sword Core after the melee; the game will let you dash out freely. Useful for getting away from an enemy's counter melee.&#x20;

Timing for this is really rough; do NOT activate Sword Core until your melee actually hits the Titan, then instantly activate, followed by a quick dash. Recommend practicing this technique a few times prior.&#x20;
