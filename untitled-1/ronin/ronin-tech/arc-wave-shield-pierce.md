# 🟢 Arc Wave Shield Pierce

Aiming for Scorch's feet is an actual thing; the feet actually stick out, and the Thermal Shield has a smaller radius than the Vortex Shield, so even when crouching, you can hit the feet. For Ion, you have to sidestep and aim for her right foot; otherwise, your Arc Wave will not pierce.
