# 🔧 Spawn Mechanics Explained (Start Here)

There are many terms in other Frontier Defense pages to shorten the information and make it easier to understand and read. Here is an overview of said spawn terms.

### <mark style="color:yellow;">Enemies Remaining</mark>

It works alongside “<mark style="color:yellow;">pool cap</mark>,” but basically, some enemies spawn only after a certain amount of enemies are killed. \
Like, for example:

<mark style="color:yellow;">97 enemies remaining; spawn Stalkers</mark> (2 units)<mark style="color:yellow;">.</mark>

Once 97 enemies remain, 2 units of stalkers will spawn in, assuming there is enough “<mark style="color:yellow;">pool cap</mark>” space.



### <mark style="color:yellow;">Enemy Values</mark>

“Unit” = 4 of a troop from a drop pod; some enemies are different. \
So 1 unit of stalkers = 4 stalkers. \
Anything that isn't a Titan or a Reaper counts as 1/4 of a unit.\
Do not mix it up with “enemies remaining.”&#x20;

* <mark style="color:yellow;">4 Grunts.</mark>&#x20;
* <mark style="color:yellow;">4 Mortar Spectres.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">4 Stalkers.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">4 Ticks.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">4 Plasma Drones.</mark>&#x20;
* <mark style="color:yellow;">1 Reaper.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">1 Titan.</mark>



### <mark style="color:yellow;">Pool Cap</mark>

The max amount (aka threshold) of units that can exist at once before a new unit can spawn in. If the unit pool cap is 5, then new units won't spawn so long as there are 5 units already spawned in.

Every so often an enemy that spawns from “<mark style="color:yellow;">remaining enemies</mark>” might temporarily increase the pool cap by 1 unit until a unit dies. So, for example, from 5 to 6.
