# 🏆 Current World Records

There is a main site where you can view all the current world record holders in Frontier Defense.

[Here's the link.](https://www.speedrun.com/titanfall_2?h=Frontier_Defense-forward-base-kodai-solo-any\&x=xd17gj4d-789kw65l.klr5prm1-wl36r0wl.5lmdk9ml-wle6mdr8.zqo5pvxl)

### <mark style="color:yellow;">Different Categories to Choose From</mark>

* <mark style="color:yellow;">Any% = It doesn't matter how much damage the Harvester takes or what difficulty.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Insane = Done on Insane difficulty only; damage is irrelevant.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Perfect (100%) = Insane difficulty only, no Titan & Pilot deaths, no Harvester damage.</mark>
