# ℹ️ Reflected Projectile Properties

## <mark style="color:yellow;">Hitscan (Vortex Bullet)</mark>

Hitscan Weapons (anything that has no travel time, hits instantly, for example, the Car SMG or Chaingun XO-16)

Respawn didn't make new values or reuse the same damage values for all weapons; that'd be too much effort to do. So instead, they decided to use a fixed universal damage value for ALL Hitscan projectiles. We call the universal bullet the “Vortex Bullet,” [even Pilot weapons, which can be exploited.](../../titan-competitive-stuff/questionable-strategies/r-97-vortex-gimmick.md)

## <mark style="color:yellow;">Projectiles</mark>

Fired-back projectiles have strange properties; they retain damage, but each projectile has a unique property. All fired back explosive projectiles will still deal self-damage.&#x20;

### <mark style="color:yellow;">**List of All Projectile Properties**</mark>

* <mark style="color:yellow;">40 mm Cannon and T-203 Launcher projectiles will be fired back inaccurately.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Railgun will be fired inaccurately too and will always deal only 250 damage regardless of charge.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">All rockets are fired accurately back, but they lose tracking if they had any to begin with.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Frags will never explode when held but will explode after 1.25 seconds of being reflected.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Firestar will extinguish in the Vortex Shield and will be reignited when thrown back. It can be infinitely reignited but can't be picked up again unless fired back by an enemy or teammate Ion.</mark>&#x20;

## <mark style="color:yellow;">Entities</mark>

### <mark style="color:yellow;">**The Following Entities Can Be Picked Up with Vortex Shield and Converted as Your Own**</mark>

* <mark style="color:yellow;">Already placed or thrown satchels can be picked up and thrown back as your own; they will explode after 1.25 seconds once thrown.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Activated and idle Tether Traps can be picked up and thrown; converted Tether Traps are immune to damage and last only 10 sec.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Ticks (only when caught in their disc-thrown form) can be caught and thrown back to deploy Ticks as your own.</mark>
