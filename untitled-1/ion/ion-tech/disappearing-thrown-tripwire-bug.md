# 🔻 Disappearing Thrown Tripwire Bug

Weird bug involving a singular Tripwire pylon glitching out and disappearing inside of a Titan, similar to the [disappearing thrown Canister bug.](../../scorch/scorch-tech/disappearing-thrown-canister-bug.md) The bug isn't that common, but essentially, it happens when you dash at a Titan while tossing Tripwire upwards. A Tripwire pylon must land inside a Titan's upper thigh region to glitch out and disappear. Only managed to make it work against Ogre chassis, probably because of their fat models.&#x20;

The thing is, this bug might be more common than people may think, since there is a max height cap to how high of an angle you can toss Tripwire at; you can't freely toss Tripwire upwards like with Thermite Canisters. But yeah, [dash is required to launch Tripwire high](long-range-tripwire-throw.md) enough to reach the upper thigh.
