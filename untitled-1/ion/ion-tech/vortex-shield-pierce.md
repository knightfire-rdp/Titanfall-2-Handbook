# 🔻 Vortex Shield Pierce

There are several ways to get through the Vortex Shield. But be wary, Vortex Shield has 2 Hitboxes, one for hitscan and one for projectiles. The projectile hitbox is a lot bigger than the hitscan one; you can't pierce the Vortex Shield directly with projectiles, unlike with hitscan.

### <mark style="color:yellow;">List of All the Ways to Pierce Vortex Shield</mark>

* <mark style="color:yellow;">Melee.</mark>
* [Thermite droplets from the T-203 Thermite Launcher.](../../scorch/scorch-tech/thermite-droplets-pierce-shield.md)
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Explosive damage.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Splash damage from a long-range Power Shot.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Electric smoke.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Angled Arc Wave aimed at an exposed foot.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Aiming for the feet directly with hitscan.</mark>

The strange Vortex Shield hitbox allows for hitscan weapons to pierce the Vortex Shield when aimed at the feet. Includes Laser Core too. Piercing the feet with Chaingun XO-16 is a lot harder because of its big spread; Legion's long-range mode, Laser Shot, and Energy Siphon are perfect for this job.
