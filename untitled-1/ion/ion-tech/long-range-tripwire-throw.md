# 🟢 Long-Range Tripwire Throw

In the code, Tripwire and [Flame Canister](../../scorch/scorch-tech/long-range-canister-throw.md) throw velocity scale with Titan velocity. Dashing and deploying Tripwire mid-dash will toss your Tripwire a much larger distance away; supposedly, you can even land Tripwire on rooftops on Colony with this technique.

Dashing + sliding off something can further increase the throw range; you can even look behind and still throw it at full speed.
