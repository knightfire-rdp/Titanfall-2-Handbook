# 🟢 Broken Rodeo Damage Glitch

While doomed, having some Titan shield, and having just a sliver of health, rodeos will barely damage you at all. Basically, making you immune to rodeo damage. No matter how much shield health you have when doomed, you'll lose barely any, though pulling this off in a game is hard. Sure, it does work with all Titans if you hit them with energy transfer, but it's mainly intended for Monarch due to it all originating from her.&#x20;

The general version of this bug for all Titans is in [rodeo-damage-explained.md](../../universal-mechanics/rodeo-damage-explained.md "mention") page.

### <mark style="color:yellow;">**Key Features**</mark>

* <mark style="color:yellow;">Damage is prioritized to shields; if you're at 50% HP, your shields will take 50% damage</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Unlike rodeo on a non-doomed Titan, rodeo damage is based off doom bar health.</mark>&#x20;
* <mark style="color:yellow;">It doesn't matter whether it's the frag grenade or battery pull animation; both deal the same doom health damage.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Can work on other Titans too with the Energy Transfer Core Upgrade.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Damage is dependent on HP amount.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Doesn't work on non-doomed Titan.</mark>
