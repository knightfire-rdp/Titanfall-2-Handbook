# ☣️ Double-Hit Energy Siphon

Hitting a specific enemy Titan model spot while the Titan is moving will cause the Energy Siphon to hit twice. The running theory is that Respawn didn't fully fit the stationary hitbox inside the moving one, Titans have 2 animations, one for moving and one for standing still. Energy Siphon by default has splash, so it probably hits the moving hitbox and then edge splashes into an exposed part of the stationary hitbox.

### <mark style="color:yellow;">**Known Info about this Glitch**</mark>

* <mark style="color:yellow;">Affects</mark> [Laser Shot](../../ion/ion-tech/double-hit-laser-shot.md) <mark style="color:yellow;">and Energy Siphon alike.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Energy Siphon gives and steals shields twice, hence the double hit.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Each Titan chassis has a slightly different spot, Prime versions too.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">The spots seem to be on the feet, 2 spots, specifically between the toes and behind the Achilles.</mark>
