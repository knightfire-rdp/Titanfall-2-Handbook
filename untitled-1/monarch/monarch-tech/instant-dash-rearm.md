# 🔰 Instant Dash Rearm

:beginner:"Ability to instantly refill your dashes at the cost of significantly longer Rearm animation."

By pressing the melee key and Rearm key together at the same time twice in a row, you can refill your dashes mid-Rearm. You save a significant amount of time in getting those dashes early at a cost of a few milliseconds longer rearm, in general.\
\
Gain dashes faster at the cost of having to wait a few milliseconds for the Rearm animation to end. Missed input will cause you to melee, resulting in you canceling the ability, meaning you have to redo it.&#x20;

### <mark style="color:yellow;">**Step-by-Step Sequence**</mark>

1. <mark style="color:yellow;">Activate Rearm.</mark>
2. <mark style="color:yellow;">Press Rearm and melee together at the same time, twice in a row as fast as possible before Rearm ends fully.</mark>
