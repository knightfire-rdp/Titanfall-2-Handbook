# 🔻 Slow Firewall Deployment

It's a very weird and random but annoying bug that for some reason exists and probably most people are victims of. The bug causes your Firewall to deploy noticably slower than normal.

You can deploy a Firewall noticeably slower by pressing and holding the Firewall key. To deploy the Firewall as fast as possible, simply press the key once (you can also use [Quickswap](../../universal-mechanics/quickswap-crouch-tech.md) to deploy it even faster).

It does not affect the speed at which Firewall fire moves; it only affects the animation for you to start the Firewall deployment.&#x20;

### <mark style="color:yellow;">Key Features</mark>

* <mark style="color:yellow;">Pressing and holding the Firewall keys causes the Firewall to deploy noticably slower.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Press the Firewall key once to deploy it as fast as possible.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Works with</mark> [Quickswap](../../universal-mechanics/quickswap-crouch-tech.md) <mark style="color:yellow;">too.</mark>
