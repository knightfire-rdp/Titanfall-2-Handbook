# ℹ️ Overlapping Canister Damage Radius

For some reason this slipped past Respawn's playtesting phase. The canister has 2 rings of thermite, the inside ring and the outer ring. The inside ring overlaps with the outside ring, so on the outside ring you get damaged by both rings, meaning you get double damaged by the canister.&#x20;

### <mark style="color:yellow;">Illustrations of the Rings</mark>

Here are some Leonardo da Vinci type illustrations of the rings. The yellow ring is the outside ring, while the red ring is the inside ring overlapping with the outside ring.
