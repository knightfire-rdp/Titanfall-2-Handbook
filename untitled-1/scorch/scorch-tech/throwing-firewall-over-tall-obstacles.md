# 🟢 Throwing Firewall Over Tall Obstacles

For some unknown reason, you can look up to throw Firewall over obstacles too tall even for Arc Wave and Flame Core. Why? No idea. But it also seems to have a reverse effect too. Looking down makes it not possible to toss your Firewall over chest-height objects, a thing not present with Arc Wave and Flame Core.

### <mark style="color:yellow;">Video Demonstration and Comparison</mark>

In the video it's a comparison of Arc Wave, Firewall, and Flame Core. The crate on top of the Crash Site hill is wonderful for this because it has several heights around the crate. You can't Arc Wave or Flame Core from the left side because the ground is slightly lower, unlike the right side. As shown in the clip, you can even cause the Firewall to not go over the crate if you look at a normal angle. Hell can even not go through the same height that other wave attacks went if you look down.
