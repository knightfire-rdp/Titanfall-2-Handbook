# ℹ️ Thermite Droplets Pierce Shield

The T-203 Thermite Launcher's projectile explodes into thermite droplets upon impact. Those thermite droplets, unlike the Flame Canister and Firewall, can, in fact, pierce defensive abilities.

Despite this, it's not recommended to shoot directly in front of the feet (except for the Particle Wall); the thermite will just slide across the ground, barely getting anywhere. It's recommended to run the Wildfire kit for that bigger spread and higher droplet count. Also advised to look for obstacles to splash against, like the wall or chest-height objects.
