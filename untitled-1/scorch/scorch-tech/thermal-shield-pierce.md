# 🔻 Thermal Shield Pierce

There are several ways to pierce through a Thermal Shield. [Unlike the Vortex Shield](../../ion/ion-tech/vortex-shield-pierce.md), the Thermal Shield has an even smaller radius, making foot shots a lot more consistent. And compared to the Vortex Shield, it doesn't have a stupidly big hitbox against projectiles, making it actually possible to pierce his shield with projectiles too.&#x20;

### <mark style="color:yellow;">List of All the Ways to Pierce Thermal Shield</mark>

* <mark style="color:yellow;">Melee.</mark>
* [Thermite droplets from the T-203 Thermite Launcher.](thermite-droplets-pierce-shield.md)
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Explosive damage.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Splash damage.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Electric smoke.</mark>
* [Angled Arc Wave aimed at an exposed foot.](../../ronin/ronin-tech/arc-wave-shield-pierce.md)
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Aiming for the feet directly.</mark>

It's not as consistent to hit the toes because of the spread, but Legion's long-range mode and Power Shots are incredible at piercing Thermal Shield.

{% embed url="https://youtu.be/Gwi3gF8yVWw" %}

Scorch can make it harder for an enemy to hit his feet by crouching, but it doesn't make him immune to it, unlike [Ion](../../ion/ion-tech/vortex-shield-pierce.md). Though, keep in mind that on top of being drastically slowed down, your top will get exposed to an enemy with keen sight. This is even easier for the enemy if they're on lower ground than you.

{% embed url="https://youtu.be/3beUZWLmIGE" %}

{% hint style="info" %}
You can use the feet hitting feature to your advantage, you could try aiming for specific parts of the foot, even against crouching, with an [Energy Siphon](../../monarch/monarch-tech/double-hit-energy-siphon.md) or [Laser Shot](../../ion/ion-tech/double-hit-laser-shot.md) to go for a double-hit.
{% endhint %}
