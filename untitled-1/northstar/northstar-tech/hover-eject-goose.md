# 🟢 Hover Eject Goose

I'm going to be real; I only know this from seeing Snowars do it on his Northstar during a match of LTS. You can goose[^1] a Pilot by hovering right above their head. Despite how absurd this is, it does work against cloaked Pilots too; a Pilot will get stuck between your feet and the Titan soul.

Titan soul is the body of a Titan that remains there for like 2 seconds after ejecting. It has a collision and is the reason I land on something during the clips. You want to position your Titan to be directly on top of the Titan and have your heels be pointed at the enemy Titan's face. The Pilot ejects directly from the face region of the Titan.

If you do it right, the Pilot will bounce off against your feet and get stuck against the Titan soul hitbox and your feet, and after a short time once Pilot's damage immunity runs out, you'll crush them. Pilots get short immunity to damage when ejecting to prevent meleeing them out of the air. Do keep in mind you'll take like nearly half a bar of damage from the eject explosion.&#x20;

If you mess up the distance, the Pilot will slip through and fall down, you can just melee them. It's going to be harder against cloaks though.

Truly one of the ways to die.&#x20;
