# 🟢 Tether Trapped Cars

It has to be one of the weirdest and cleverest use cases of Tether Trap I've ever seen. You can place a Tether Trap inside of a car, the ones on Boomtown (probably other maps have them too). The cars are strange and seem to behave like a fully solid entity despite having holes you can throw Tether Trap or shoot through. But this seems apparent when trying to destroy a placed Tether Trap via e-smoke or explosives. The explosives and e-smoke are unable to break the Tether Trap, definitely useful for dealing with Sword Core.

There are certainly more use cases of this since some small windows don't allow e-smoke to pass through either, while Tether Trap can see the Titan and attach itself. Those destroyed cars be found on Boomtown and Angel City.

### <mark style="color:yellow;">**Key Features**</mark> <a href="#key-features" id="key-features"></a>

* <mark style="color:yellow;">Hidden from sight.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Immune to e-smoke and</mark> [<mark style="color:yellow;">AoE</mark>](#user-content-fn-1)[^1] <mark style="color:yellow;">(sometimes Cluster Missile sub-explosions can slip inside).</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">So long as the Tether Trap is even partially in the car, it'll work.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Arc Wave, Firewall, and Flame Core can break it only from certain angles.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">The Tether Trap can still be shot or destroyed by explosions inside the car; be mindful of that.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">You can even deal direct impact damage with stuff like Cluster Missile if you get an angle.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">The Tether Trap works on</mark> [<mark style="color:yellow;">LoS</mark>](#user-content-fn-2)[^2] <mark style="color:yellow;">to attach itself to a Titan.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Has a ton of potential against Sword Core.</mark>
