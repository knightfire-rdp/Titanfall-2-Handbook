### <mark style="color:yellow;">Hover Strafing</mark> \[0:07-0:56]

Hover strafing is by far the most important and useful hover mechanic to know; it allows the user to reach max hover speed and turn effectively while the Railgun is charged. Letting you travel what appears to look like double the distance that a regular hover would give you. People normally dash to accelerate a hover; this lets you save that dash while letting you turn/dodge shots better.&#x20;

#### <mark style="color:yellow;">Key Features</mark>

* <mark style="color:yellow;">Reach max hover speed and turn effectively while the Railgun is charged.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Increases max Hover distance.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Can replace “dash into hover” for acceleration.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Viper Thrusters are affected in a very similar way.</mark>&#x20;

To perform this mechanic, simply move in a serpentine-like movement (diagonal curves) while hovering. It's a lot better on controller than on MKB[^1].&#x20;

### <mark style="color:yellow;">Crouch Hover</mark> \[0:57-1:16]

A semi-useful mechanic that has no purpose in combat but lets you reach Hover spots easier and even lets you reach otherwise impossible Hover spots too. Quite simply, hold crouch at any point during Hover to partially retract your legs. It's a bit similar to how planes retract their landing gear during flight. But unlike planes, you don't get any better aerodynamics by retracting your legs during Hover.

#### <mark style="color:yellow;">Key Features</mark>

* <mark style="color:yellow;">Makes reaching Hover spots easier.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Makes some otherwise impossible Hover spots possible.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Does not aid you in combat and movement.</mark>





### <mark style="color:yellow;">Flight Core Stacking</mark> \[1:17-1:55]

It is one of the more useful mechanics that not only helps you with reaching Hover spots but also helps you in combat. Basically, by activating Flight Core shortly after Hover ends, you'll be able to lift yourself a bit higher, [literally out of Sword Core range](/broken/pages/E6l7xNUwyA5asD602aLw), which normally is not possible. This does not work vice versa, and be aware that you can jam your thrusters.<sup><mark style="color:orange;">**⟨1⟩**<mark style="color:orange;"></sup>

You can actually force [Ground Core (Turkey Core)](hover-mechanics.md#ground-core-turkey-core-1-56-2-19) by activating Hover and then immediately activating Flight Core to stay near to the ground. It might be useful on maps with tall obstacles like Glitch and Complex.

#### <mark style="color:yellow;">Key Features</mark>

* <mark style="color:yellow;">Ability to fly higher and reach higher Hover spots.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Ability to dodge Sword Core.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Ability to force activate Ground Core</mark> (Turkey Core)<mark style="color:yellow;">.</mark>

{% hint style="danger" %}
<sup><mark style="color:orange;">**⟨1⟩**<mark style="color:orange;"></sup>You can jam your thrusters by activating Flight Core during Hover or even too early after Hover ends. Jamming causes you to stay stuck at current height, so if you jam your thrusters mid hover before you even leave the ground, you'll be stuck at that height. Same thing for when you're already airborne, you'll stay stuck at that height.

You need to wait a second before activating Flight Core after Hover ends, you'll know when you'll feel your Titan starting to fall.
{% endhint %}





### <mark style="color:yellow;">Ground Core</mark> (Turkey Core) \[1:56-2:19]

A rather silly-looking mechanic that has some uses. You can Flight Core from the ground via 2 methods, by either [jamming your thrusters](hover-mechanics.md#flight-core-stacking-1-17-1-55) early during Hover or by activating Flight Core below an obstacle preventing you from rising, like a doorway. Once grounded, you'll be unable to fly up, even if you leave the obstacle blocking your height.

Due to how silly it looks, it's sometimes called Turkey Core, due to how turkeys can't fly, so Northstar looks like a big turkey walking around. This does let you get some more usage out of Flight Core on maps with many high obstacles that you could get stuck on, like Complex or Glitch. Though be warned, being on the ground makes you vulnerable to thermite, Arc Waves, executions, etc.

#### <mark style="color:yellow;">Key Features</mark>

* <mark style="color:yellow;">Ability to ground yourself to the ground during Flight Core.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Either jam your thrusters or Flight Core below an obstacle to ground yourself.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Adds more versatility to maps with many air obstacles, like Complex and Glitch.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Has multiple names, even “Turkey Core.”</mark>





### <mark style="color:yellow;">Hover Walking</mark> \[2:20-2:45]

A very silly thing that makes your Northstar visually airwalk during the whole hover animation. Outside of dumbfounding your opponents with this bizarre move skill, you won't get anything out of it. It's intended just as a joke. This does not work with Flight Core (thank God).

To perform this mechanic, simply press and hold crouch before you activate Hover and keep holding it until the end of the duration. You'll be the next airwalking prodigy if you practice it enough.
