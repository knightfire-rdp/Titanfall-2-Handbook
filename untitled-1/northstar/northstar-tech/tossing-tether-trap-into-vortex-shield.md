# 🟢 Tossing Tether Trap into Vortex Shield

Well it's already part of the [vortex-reflect-delay.md](../../ion/ion-tech/vortex-reflect-delay.md "mention") page. But I decided to add this since it's essential for Northstar players to be aware of this. But basically, you can toss your Tether Trap into a Vortex Shield to guarantee your next shot to hit the Ion. This, of course works with other projectiles and even hitscan, but Northstar can literally just toss a Tether Trap without breaking her charge, which is what makes it so important.
